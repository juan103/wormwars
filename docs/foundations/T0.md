## 1. Compute accounting

**The units, per category:**
- **World simulation:** counted in `World` itself.
  - Worlds built: `World.__init__`.
  - **World-ticks actually simulated:** `World.tick`. `run` stops early when every world is
    done, and only the ticks that ran count.
- **Neural updates:** counted in `Brain.step`, as **S × B × effective substeps** network updates.
  - B is the batch axis: worlds × weys in a rollout, 1 in the input-response and history probes.
  - Padding and dead slots that are computed still count.
  - The `substeps` override is respected.
  - Scripted brains count zero neural updates.
  - `scripts/bench_brain.py`'s `SparseBrain` bypasses `Brain.step`; it is a benchmark and is
    listed as uncounted.
- **Why these choke points:** twelve places build a `World` (Fable, Astra), including
  calibration, the exp02 behaviour probes, exp03 coverage and its stimulus bank, 02b's replays,
  coevolution, geometry, the viewers and the benchmarks. `_play` sees only some of them. Counting
  in `World` and `Brain` needs no list of exceptions.
  - **A test checks** that every production path listed above records into the ledger.

**Categories, and how they are counted:**
- The categories are selection, holdout, snapshot, final, calibration, probe, tuning and other.
- Categories are set by a context manager. Nesting is resolved to the innermost category, so
  nothing is counted twice.
- A holdout evaluation triggered by a snapshot is one evaluation, counted once.
- **Test:** `evolve` puts nothing in "other".

**Time:**
- recorded as **synchronised wall time per category** (`torch.cuda.synchronize()` at category
  boundaries), and labelled that way. It is not GPU kernel time.
- It works on CPU, and the category is restored on exceptions. Both are tested.

**Where the counts go:**
- `RunResult.compute` holds a run's counts. The old `evaluations` field is kept unchanged
  (selection only), so existing records stay comparable, and is documented as such.
- **Experiment level:** each experiment script writes a `compute.json` next to its results,
  summing every process. That includes calibration attempts and retries, probes and tuning.
  exp03 never calls `evolve`, so this is where its accounting lives.
- **Test:** counts equal hand-computed counts for a small `evolve` run, a calibration and a probe.

## 2. Genome and score pairing

- **Order and chunking:** a permuted population, and any `chunk_worlds`, give the same **full
  [strain, world] score arrays**, permuted identically. This holds in each execution mode's own
  terms (section 4).
- **Stochastic probes must pair too. Jitter currently does not** (Astra, reproduced).
  - The jitter generator is seeded once per run and draws noise by batch position. Reordering
    strains, or changing chunks, changes their sensory noise: one per-world score moved by 0.0053.
  - **Fix:** noise keyed by world id, tick and individual, as common random numbers across
    strains.
  - **The regression test first.** The fix changes jitter draws, so future jitter results are not
    bit-comparable with 02's. 02's jitter estimates are unaffected in expectation: each is an
    average over independent noise.
- **Serialised scores:**
  - **A latent mismatch.** `save_population` stores `fitness` = one best score *per generation*
    beside the final *population*. Nothing reads it yet, but it is not paired with the genomes.
    **Fix:** save the final per-strain scores as `fitness`, and the per-generation history under
    an explicit name. Test that they are index-aligned.
  - **The champion.** It is chosen by a second evaluation (`evolve.py:171-174`), on the same
    worlds as the last generation. Under default CUDA that can pick a different strain than the
    log's best. **Fix:** count it as "final", and test that the champion's genome equals the one
    the log names (by genome hash, not nickname) in each execution mode. Or reuse the last `fit`,
    as an equivalence-checked change recorded in DECISIONS.md. The choice is made with the test.
  - Snapshots and holdout values are tested for pairing to their genomes too.

## 3. Complete inheritance

- **`Genome.cat` and `Genome.assign`,** which iterate over the tensor fields. Every hand-built
  `Genome(...)` outside `brain.py` moves to them: `evolve.py`, `coevolve.py` (two of which drop
  `dale_sign`), `exp02/probes.py` and `deletion.py`.
- **A scan test** fails if new code builds `Genome(...)` outside `brain.py`.
- **The inheritance test enumerates the fields itself,** with distinguishable values in every field
  (initial gaps are identical across strains, so they are overwritten), and Dale on and off.
- **Operations covered:** `select`, `clone`, `mutate` (unmutated fields unchanged), `breed` (elites
  and children), `_breed_islands`, `_migrate`, `cat`, `assign`, save and load. The test also
  checks that outputs do not alias their parents.
- **Integrity on load:** `genome_sha256`, which is written already, is checked when loading. The
  hash is extended to cover `dale_sign`.

## 4. Save, load and replay: three separate claims

1. **Round trip:** save then load preserves every tensor **exactly**, on any device.
2. **Re-simulation, same device and mode:**
   - exact on CPU;
   - exact on CUDA under the repo's defined exact mode, `replay_mode()` (`evo/bundle.py`,
     `docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md`).
3. **Historical replay:** an old champion replays to the score in its log **on the device and in
   the mode recorded in its bundle**. For published runs that is CUDA. A replay on another device
   is not expected to be exact.

**Default (non-deterministic) CUDA: a tolerance declared per comparison, before measuring.**
- **Comparison:** the same genomes and worlds run twice on one GPU, and the same set under two
  chunkings.
- **Statistic:** the per-world score, over the full episode (600 ticks at T0 and T1 settings).
- **Genomes:** 32 random genomes and 8 evolved champions (02's).
- **Worlds:** 16.
- **Repeats:** 3.
- **Hardware:** the one RTX 5080, with the pinned environment recorded.
- **Declared bound:** per-world |Δscore| ≤ **1e-4** for random genomes. For champions, the bound is
  also 1e-4, *provisionally*. The repo documents long-horizon divergence under default CUDA
  (`REPRODUCIBILITY.md`), and 02 measured a chaos floor of 0.0018 on an aggregate statistic.
- **Exceeding the bound is a finding, not a failure to hide.** It is recorded with its
  distribution, and the claim becomes "replay within X only under replay_mode". The bound is never
  widened silently.

## 5. Energy ledger and numerical checks

- **The energy-ledger bound, declared now:** relative error below **1e-5**, per world against its
  own starting energy, **at every tick**. That is the documented acceptance bound in
  `tests/test_world.py`.
  - `_play` currently returns only the final absolute residual, so it gains the per-tick maximum
    of the normalised error.
  - Residuals are checked for finiteness before aggregation, because `max(worst, NaN)` can keep an
    earlier finite value.
- **Numbers:** no NaN or Inf in scores, energies or brain states over a full episode. This is
  checked for random genomes at the mutation bounds, the extreme genomes in `tests/test_brain.py`,
  and evolved champions.
- **The suite:** N2, SH and RD, 8 worlds each, at T0 and T1 settings.

## 6. Island follow-ups (should-fix from the review of D064)

- **Validate supported island settings** at the top of `evolve`. Empty islands, `migrate_every`
  = 0, and `migrants` at least an island's size are all errors; an uneven population no longer
  gives a shape mismatch (Astra reproduced one with 5 strains, 2 islands and 3 migrants).
- **Document the per-island elite and truncation rule,** which changes total selection pressure.
- **Stronger tests:**
  - a migrant survives breeding with every field intact;
  - all ring edges, and the untouched slots;
  - isolation over several generations (with zero sigma, every slot descends from its own
    island);
  - the log and snapshots still pair.
- **A single-island regression against a stored published log:** the first 2-3 generations of a
  published run, from its bundle config and seed, on its recorded device. This runs after 03r
  frees the GPU.
- **D064's wording is corrected** (Astra, Fable):
  - the stale Dale vector corrupts later mutation and clamping, not the migrant's immediate
    forward pass;
  - a stale migrant fitness leads to culling when truncation excludes the slot, not invariably;
  - the single-island evidence is Astra's parent-versus-current run, not the tautological
    `breed` comparison.
