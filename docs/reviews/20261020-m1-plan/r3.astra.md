1. **High — bootstrap ignores shared randomness across graphs.** [summarise.py:115](/D:/Claude/random/wormWars/scripts/memory_m1/summarise.py:115) resamples genomes independently for each graph. However, all seed-7600 panels use the same generator seed and dimensions: I checked that N2 and all 30 sampled graphs have 3,709 chemical edges. `Genome.random` therefore shares the random sign draws, taus and biases across corresponding genome indices. Independent resampling destroys this dependence; the resulting intervals do not reproduce the sampling design. **Fix:** resample genome indices jointly across seed-7600 panels, independently for N2’s seed-7700 panel, while retaining graph resampling within ensembles. Alternatively, specify independent graph-specific seeds before running.
