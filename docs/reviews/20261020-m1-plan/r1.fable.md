**Verdict: revise.** The definitions match the code, the identities are exact, and the coverage is complete. Three things need fixing before the run: one control cannot fail, and both decision rules can fire or fail for reasons unrelated to their question.

**What checked out**

- Share, eligibility (a ≥ 1e-3), remaining (|δy(300)| > 0.1a; `end = d[-1]` is tick 300 under follow=300), settled (full-state range over the last 10 ticks < 1e-4): all match `scripts/p4mc.py:549-557` and `twin.py:22-35, 81-89`.
- The read-out is ±1/4 on the eight neurons named (`configs/interface.yaml:60-61`, `measures.py:84-86`), so |δy| = ‖c‖‖δa‖|cos θ| is exact.
- A1 and B1 are exact on the eligible set; the log split is trivially exact given share = N·A.
- 99 mirror pairs (`configs/mirror_pairs.yaml`, counted). The array files exist with the plan's names.
- Every roadmap M1 bullet and M3's predictors are present.

**Findings**

1. **High. B4, tau controls.** The tau-permutation arm is vacuous as a population kill. `brain.py:235-239` draws taus iid per neuron, log-uniform, independent of the weights. Permuting a genome's taus across neurons is a draw from the same joint distribution, so "N2's lead survives" holds by construction, up to noise. It costs about an hour and cannot fire. Fix: drop it as a kill, or keep it only as a per-genome pairing diagnostic (correlation of r_i between intact and permuted). The equal-tau arm is the real control; keep it.

2. **Medium. D2's per-graph ratio.** log(A_N2/A_G) / log(share_N2/share_G) is arbitrary in sign and size when share_G is near or above share_N2. In `measurements.json`, 5 null graphs have a tick-30 share at or above 0.44 and 43 are between 0.35 and 0.43, and N2's fresh-panel share moves between 0.40 and 0.50. With 20 random graphs, drawing one near N2 is likely. The median over 20 is robust to a few, but those graphs' values are not "a fraction of the excess". Fix: compute on aggregates per ensemble, log(A_N2 / median A_G) / log(share_N2 / median share_G), or exclude graphs with log(share_N2/share_G) < 0.1 and report how many.

3. **Medium. B1/D2, the split is one convention.** N = Σ w_i n_i is "the share with alignment frozen at tick 0". The alternative, A′ = Σ w_i α_i ("the share with the norm frozen"), N′ = share/A′, is equally valid and differs when n_i and α_i covary across genomes. D2 could flip between them. Fix: report both, and let D2 fire only if both exceed 0.5. Also define the summands as products (‖δa_i(t)‖|cos θ_i(t)|, ‖δa_i(t)‖|cos θ_i(0)|) rather than via α_i, to avoid 0/0, and say "all eligible genomes".

4. **Medium. D1, three gaps.**
   - "Median random null graph" is unspecified: per ensemble or pooled over 640? 03mc compares per ensemble (medians 0.255 to 0.291). State that f is per ensemble and "vanishes" needs f < 0.25 in every ensemble.
   - No replication. D2 requires N2's second panel; D1 does not. On B3 the denominator may be 0.1 to 0.2 and f's noise can cross 0.25. Require both N2 panels.
   - Standardising on a alone. a = ‖c‖‖δa(0)‖|cos θ(0)| confounds size with alignment, so "matched a" can pair large N2 state differences with small null ones. A2 cannot do better (the arrays hold only the read-out difference, `p4mc.py:559-562`), but B3 has the states. Add a ‖δa(0)‖-standardised share on B3 and say which one D1 uses.

5. **Medium. A2's strata.** The top decile is open-ended, and that is where N2's excess sits (03mc: the largest contributors carry an unusual part). Within it, N2's genomes may be far larger than the nulls', so the "matched" comparison is not matched where it matters. Sparse deciles (not only empty ones) also make E[|δy(30)| | k] noisy. Fix: sub-cut the top decile (90-95, 95-99, 99-100), require a minimum count per stratum, and report per-graph E[a | k] so a mismatch is visible. Same for B3's pooled strata.

6. **Medium. C1 and C3, |K| may be most of the graph.** R's two-step in-neighbourhood runs through premotor hubs and may cover most of the ~300 neurons. Then C1 is near global density, and C3 ("degree inside K") is near a hub list, which batch 7 found does not carry the lead (0.343). Fix: report |K| on N2 and per ensemble; pre-commit a fallback (one-step neighbourhood) if |K| exceeds half the graph; report C3's overlap with batch 7's hub set and with top30_start.

7. **Low. B4, label slip.** The plan says top30_start was "chosen from N2's own residual". `batch6.py:73-74`: top30_start ranks neurons by mean tick-0 activity difference; top30_residual is the tick-30 one. Both are result-defined, but fix the wording.

8. **Low. B4, gap ratio.** `g` is not per genome: `brain.py:228-233` expands one anatomical vector over all strains, so L_G is per graph. The step is Laplacian in form (`brain.py:450, 482-483`), so the Rayleigh quotient is well defined, but the true dissipation rate is δvᵀT⁻¹Lδv with T = diag(τ). Say the measure is τ-free, or report both.

9. **Low. B4, the forecast observable.** `batch3.py:53-58` projects the voltage with c. The tanh correction is one line (multiply c by 1 − tanh²(m)). Apply it here rather than waiting for M2, or state that M1's forecast is the voltage one.

10. **Low. Undefined kill wording.** "N2's lead survives" (tau), "N2's antisymmetric part no slower" (needs the antisymmetric fraction at ticks 0 and 30, or its retention), "under 2%" (which graph). Define the lead as share_N2 minus the median of the 20, and "survives" as, say, at least half the intact lead.

11. **Low. Cost.** The README gives 2 minutes per 1024 genomes for 210 ticks, about 0.57 s per tick. 32 panels × 410 ticks is about 2.1 h; the tau arms about 1 h; five Jacobian panels about 0.5 h; the 16-row ramp-end batch about 0.3 h. Nearer 4 h than 2 to 3. Finding 1 removes the hour that matters least.

12. **Low. B2 at 1024 genomes.** N2's 03mc settled rate is 26 of 1977, so expect about 13 in N2 and a median of 0 in the nulls. The fresh settled fractions will be few-genome noise. Fine as a proxy; say so and let A5 carry M2's estimate.

13. **Low. Coverage detail.** The roadmap asks for the decomposition "overall and within amplitude strata". A2 gives per-decile shares but not E[r] + Cov/E[a] per decile. Add it; it costs seconds.

14. **Low. D2 on all genomes.** The roadmap says "in the genomes that carry the signal"; the plan decides on all eligible genomes with a-weighting and reports the top quartile. Defensible, since w_i already emphasises the carriers, but say why the decision uses the full set.
