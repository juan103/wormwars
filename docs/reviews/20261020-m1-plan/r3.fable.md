1. **Medium-high, B3/D1 (plan lines 118-122; `summarise.py:161-184`). Coverage will likely fail because of N2's own k ≥ 1 arms.** The pooled population holds about 84k null observations and 8k from N2. By the plan's own facts, N2 at k = 1 and k = 2 sits at a ≈ 0.1 to 0.2 while the nulls' k = 2 arm averages about 0.05. The top-1% stratum (~900 observations) will be nearly all N2 at k = 2, so some null panel will have fewer than 10 there and it is dropped; the 95-99 stratum is at risk too. Those strata hold N2's largest |δy(30)| values, plausibly over 25% of its pooled numerator. D1 then reads "inconclusive (conditions)" for a structural reason that more graphs cannot cure. The informative matched region is N2 at k ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 1} against the nulls at k ∈ {0.5, 1, 2}. Fix, before running: either restrict each side to those three arms (eligibility at every one of its own arms, equal weight per arm), or keep four arms but judge coverage and balance on N2's intact-arm numerator and denominator. State the choice in the plan.
