## Must fix before publishing

1. **The "why it passed anyway" explanation is wrong** (`RESULTS.md:62-67`). The rank test is scale-free. Whether N2 tops an ensemble depends only on its position in latent-SD units and on the shape of the ensemble's upper tail, not on the absolute size of the latent SD. The write-up quotes z of 2.2 to 2.5 and a registered power of 0.00 to 0.33 at those z, then says it passed because three ensembles' SDs were smaller than the pilot's. That is a non sequitur: the §7 table is already indexed by z. The real reason is visible in `report.json`: the P4 distributions are bounded and left-skewed with a compressed upper tail. SH-class (values at `report.json:1868-1997`) has a mean of 0.825, a maximum of 0.907 (1.6 latent SDs above the mean) and a floor of 0.596 (4.7 SDs below). The low outliers inflate the SD estimate, so the quoted z understates how far N2 sits above the bulk, and the Gaussian power simulation of §7 assumed a symmetric tail that these ensembles do not have. Under a Gaussian at the observed z values, the chance of r ≤ 1 in all five ensembles is roughly 1%; the skew makes it far more likely. My precision review (`docs/reviews/20260925-222802-03-precision/fable.answer.md:30`) flagged exactly this. Rewrite the bullet: the rank test is exact regardless of shape, the Gaussian power table was pessimistic for a bounded ratio, and the margin was never binding (see next item).

2. **State that the verdict rests on the rank test alone and on exchangeability with a null that also permutes weights** (`RESULTS.md:36-45`, `218-219`). Every margin (0.018 to 0.037) is far below every effect-interval lower end (0.077 to 0.170), so the latent SD estimates affect the margin and the quoted z but could not change any verdict. Say so. Then say what §2 says and RESULTS never does: the ensembles differ from N2 in topology *and* weight placement, and the p-values are exact only under an exchangeability assumption that finite swap chains from N2 approximate. "A property none of the five ensembles reproduces" should be read as "none of these degree-preserving rewirings with permuted weights reproduces". The descriptive N2perm1 (0.797, ordinary rank) shows that re-placing the weights alone can remove the property in one of three draws, so the sentence at line 218 should not read as a claim about wiring.

3. **N2-rev is used as evidence without its denominator** (`RESULTS.md:167-170`, `193-194`). Its M0 common-mode response is 9e-5, below the P1 floor. Its history-probe steady contrast passed the 1e-4 floor, but the write-up does not give it. A ratio of two numbers near 1e-4 from tanh differences can be numerically fragile, and the "argues against a pure drive explanation" and "responsiveness and persistence separate" claims lean on it. Report N2-rev's mean |final| and mean |steady contrast| in the decomposition table, or drop N2-rev from the argument and rest it on N2perm2 alone.

4. **Replicate before any public claim.** The pass condition is discrete: r ≤ 1 in every ensemble. It was met with r = 1 in SH-route (graph at 0.9337 versus N2 at 0.9309, `report.json:1794`) and SH-route's top five are all above 0.905. With 128 graphs the top order statistic is a noisy estimate of the tail, and N2 itself is not the source of uncertainty (SE 0.006). Draw fresh SH-route and SH-mirror ensembles at minimum, ideally all five with new seeds, and pre-register the same rule. At 111 s per graph that is about 4 GPU-hours per ensemble of 128. I would run 256 for SH-route since one extra graph flips the result.

## Should fix

5. **Headline wording** (`RESULTS.md:3-4`, `218`, `235`). "Carry more of their recent input" invites the responsiveness reading that the exploratory section then has to argue against. P4 as defined in §5 measures how much of the difference set by the *earlier* food level survives once inputs converge, which for a linear system is a pure function of relaxation time over the 10-tick ramp. "Retain more of the food level they came from" or "relax more slowly toward the new input" is the plain reading. "Than the brains of any of five null ensembles do" also reads as "than any ensemble graph", which one SH-route graph contradicts.

6. **Calibration cannot enter P4, but say so** (`RESULTS.md:143-147`). Both `wormwars/exp03/measures.py:84` and the 02b probe take the raw read-out before the motor gain and clip, and the input gains are fixed config, so the calibrated gains touch neither P4 nor the raw magnitudes. A reader of "each graph is calibrated" will ask. The one artefact route that remains is tanh saturation of the read-out neurons under N2's 5 to 7 times larger drive: saturation compresses the steady contrast more than the intermediate final state and would inflate the ratio. The write-up lists saturation as a mechanism candidate, but it is also a measurement-artefact candidate. A cheap local check is the correlation of per-genome ratio with per-genome |steady contrast| within N2, and the same ratio on the motor read-out.

7. **The 02b comparison omits the ensembles' own ratios** (`RESULTS.md:178-186`). The evolved champions' 0.90 to 0.95 is compared with N2's 0.93, but the random shuffles here already sit at 0.75 to 0.84 on the same ratio. The champions are only modestly above unselected shuffles, and 02b's generation-0 ratio is not given at all. That weakens "selection amplified a property N2 already has" and should be said. Also 0.88 is a median of per-genome decays, not an average (`report.py:102`).

8. **Correction 2 to experiment 02 overstates** (`RESULTS.md:197`, `206-208`). "Readings that do not hold at generation 0" is the heading, but 02's claim was about best-of-32 champions on T0 and T1, and 03's P2 is a mean over unselected genomes on T1 only. That is a different population and task, not a failed replication. Reword as "does not extend to unselected random brains".

9. **Discreteness framing** (`RESULTS.md:11-12`, `59-61`). "Just inside the threshold (0.047 against 0.05)" describes the p-value grid, not closeness in the usual sense: the attainable values are 0.023, 0.047, 0.070. The fragility bullet already says one graph decides it, which is the honest statement; drop the "just inside" numeric framing or explain the grid.

**Overall judgement.** This is an accurate and honest report of the pre-registered experiment: the numbers are right, the rules were applied as written, the two failed predictions are reported alongside the one that passed, and the exploratory work is fenced off. It is not yet publishable, because the fragility section explains the pass with a reason that is statistically wrong, and because the registered section omits the two facts a sceptical reader needs first: the verdict rests entirely on N2 being above 127 or 128 of 128 in five bounded, left-skewed distributions, and the null it beats permutes weights as well as wiring. With those fixes, the N2-rev denominator, the softened 02 and 02b comparisons, and a pre-registered replication on fresh ensemble draws, I would be comfortable with the owner being asked whether to publish.
