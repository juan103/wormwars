**Revise.** The engineering question is worthwhile, but v1 can fail because its isolated controller cannot propel itself, or succeed through the direct reflex path without demonstrating a useful ring. Stage B also does not yet distinguish retention, redundancy and erosion.

Must-fix items:

1. **Stage A — supply locomotion and define the carrier.** Zeroing N2’s weights, conductances and biases gives **zero commanded forward motion**: the graft reaches only the turn neurons; forward uses separate AVB/PVC versus AVA/AVD/AVE sets. Give both modules the same declared forward drive and fixed motor-relay time constants. An isolated background is then a valid component test. Add a separate integration check on random N2 and the chosen champion, measuring forward drive, turn bias and individual motor saturation. Passing isolation does not establish a working generation-0 graft. See [motor readout](/D:/Claude/random/wormWars/wormwars/world.py:742).

2. **Modules — repair the bearing/turn-copy argument.** The cosine mapping produces approximately
   `tan ψ = [(L−R)/(L+R)] tan φ`.
   For the Gaussian, that ratio encodes lateral displacement, **not physical bearing independently of distance**. Rotating this representation by the animal’s angular velocity therefore needs validation, not analogy. Moreover, T_L/T_R activity is not the executed turn after motor filtering, N2 drive and clipping. Specify and calibrate the feedback against actual `0.30 × turn`, including its delay, or explicitly describe it as an approximate internal command. Test both directions, multiple distances and imposed background turns. [Goulard’s model](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1009383) uses retinotopic visual azimuth and self-motion input; it does not establish this chemical-input transformation.

3. **Stage A — make the gate test the owner’s ring.** Keep 5.0 targets as an engineering acceptance threshold, with a specified uncertainty rule. Keep E2d’s operational “uses” criterion: both paired contrasts’ 95% lower bounds exceed 0.5. **Remove gain ≥32 as a mandatory behavioral gate**; a useful nonlinear or history-dependent controller need not have that static slope. Promote bump formation, persistence, anchoring and correctly signed rotation to component acceptance checks. Include a direct-path lesion and a shifter/turn-copy ablation: otherwise M2 can pass entirely through NL/NR→T. M0 also needs demonstrated competence before B2 can support “the ring earns its neurons.”

4. **Stage A tuning — expand the relevant dimensions.** Include forward drive, baseline/search-turn bias, recurrence strength, global inhibition, relevant time constants, shifter gain, and direct-path strength, alongside nose gain, φ and output weights. Use a bounded staged grid rather than their full Cartesian product. **E1’s k=32 score of 5.63 used turn bias 0.2; at zero bias it scored 4.23.** Omitting that dimension makes the proposed justification misleading. Test zero/weak scent through the 0.35 peak, sign reversals and target relocation. The report’s “tanh stays linear near 1” is inaccurate: its derivative there is about 0.42. Its claimed settling and persistence times are also unvalidated.

5. **Gain probe / “What is known” — retain the measurement, narrow the inference.** The implementation is sound for its stated static, zero-start, zero-other-input experiment, and I independently recomputed median |k| **0.097839**, maximum **0.681203**. It supports **weak sustained differential response under those conditions**, not “gain rather than topology” as the cause of the plateau, nor a demonstrated temporal mechanism. Amend the claims accordingly; “all sit near 2.2” also repeats wording already corrected in E2d.

   Extend the probe with signed response curves, transient responses, multiple initial/history states, realistic collision inputs, and individual motor voltages/derivatives. Save raw responses, genome/config/code hashes and accounting. Averaged net turn below saturation does not exclude saturated individual neurons or oscillations. Use small central differences separately from large-signal tests: on the existing grid, even an ideal clipped **k=256** controller fits an OLS slope of only **66.7**.

6. **Stage B — make initialization and B3 genuinely controlled.** Generate the original 302-neuron populations first, then transplant their parameters by neuron/edge identity. Calling `Genome.random()` on different extended graphs changes anatomical normalization and RNG consumption; identical seeds do not preserve backgrounds. Define whether B4 starts with clones or perturbations of the champion.

   B3’s outputs must remain masked throughout evolution, rather than merely starting at zero and regrowing under mutation. Preserve the same parameterization and mutation policy where possible; verify no outgoing chemical or gap influence. Call it **“no added module output”**, since native N2 still receives stereo inputs. Its module undergoes neutral inheritance under a selected background, including hitchhiking—not a pure mutation-only random walk.

7. **Stage B outcomes — separate usefulness, persistence and erosion.** B1−B3 ≥0.5 needs a registered interval/test over independent runs. Six of eight is an operational count, not strong evidence of reliable retention; even against a 50% success null its one-sided binomial p is 0.145.

   Measure retention at fixed generations and in the final population, separately from the best archived champion. E2’s champion can come from generation 0 or an early checkpoint. Replace “lesion cost halves ⇒ eroded”: the initial cost can be zero/negative, and declining cost can indicate redundancy or transfer into N2. Use standardized module transplants/reset-to-seed probes to distinguish lost module function from reduced dependence. Define the generation-0 comparator and handling of failed starts. Evaluate Stage A’s frozen controller on the **same final hold-out** for “beyond the module.”

8. **Engine — narrow the “new code, not a change” claim.** The neural/world dynamics support variable neuron counts, but the current GA initializer and mutation path do not directly implement seeded populations and parameter-block scales. Those additions need equivalence checks too. `BrainSpec` constructs chemical edges from `chem > 0`: inhibitory designed weights belong in `Genome.w`, with a positive connectivity mask.

   Compare against the previous implementation/commit, including trajectories, currents, motors, scores and unchanged mutation behavior. Test the actual training and single-champion batch shapes. Changing matrix dimension can alter floating-point reductions on either device; CPU exactness must be demonstrated. Include sabotage checks for the inert cut and probe targeting.

Suggestions:

- **Arms and replication:** Prefer 16 paired runs for B1–B3, subject to measured throughput. B4 is relevant to gluing, but one chosen champion is one background. Either add a matched champion-only continuation or keep B4 descriptive. A frozen-module evolutionary comparator is more informative about the consequences of freeing the module than another unpaired background.
- **Mutation:** Separate scales are defensible. Specify module `w=0.02`, `g=0.01`, log-τ `0.0375`, bias `0.0125`, including ownership of bridge weights and motor-relay parameters. Check offspring functional survival before freezing the choice. Neither E2d nor the literature establishes 0.25× as optimal. Interface gains remain fixed unless explicitly made evolvable.
- **M1 and `hold`:** Include M1 as a Stage A comparator/ablation, not another full evolutionary arm yet. Keep `hold` secondary. It continuously supplies stale readings, which can re-anchor the ring incorrectly, and also delays relocation information; it is not a clean memory test. Start with controlled cue-off/rotation probes. A recurrent M0 is not automatically memoryless.
- **Budget:** A 24-hour cap within 96 is sensible. Correct the projection: dense matrix work scales approximately as `(332/302)² = 1.209`, not 1.10; actual rollout overhead requires measurement. Reserve time explicitly for probes, failures and 16-run evaluation.
- **Biology:** “Fly-inspired engineering” is honest. Keep “no published model found” scoped to the search. The temporal klinotaxis framing is supported by [Izquierdo and Lockery](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3422662/); I could not verify the specific 8 µm claim in that cited paper. Avoid presenting the simplified readout as a faithful PFL3 port: [Westeinde’s model](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10881397/) combines heading, goal input and nonlinear responses.
