**Verdict: go to v2 with these changes.** The question is well posed and arm L is a fair test, provided the fitness denominator, the tie handling and the "selection could see the gradient" reading are fixed before the pilot. Without those, a null is uninterpretable.

**Findings**

1. **(High) The fitness denominator is undeclared, and only "over all trials" works.** §3 says "real-first share over its generation's trials". Over entries, a strain that stalls on every trial but one and enters real scores 1.0, so selection rewards stalling. Over all trials, X0 scores 0.5 × its entry rate, a decoy entry and a non-entry both score 0, and breaking B is penalised. Fix: fitness = real-first count / 2W, as the feasibility check's selection rule (`readout`'s `real_first_all`). No performance tie-breaker: any term on entry rate or time-to-entry selects the links for something other than the cue, and under pairing that term is symmetric across assignments so it carries no cue information anyway.

2. **(High) Ties are the normal case, and `breed` breaks them by index.** Under pairing, every strain whose links tip no maze pair scores exactly 0.5. Most small-factor children do. `breed` uses `np.argsort(-fitness)` with the default unstable sort, so ties are resolved by an arbitrary deterministic order that puts the three elites (E-G exact, at indices 0-2) back in the top 8 every generation. Fix: break ties at random from the run's generator (a lexsort on fitness then a random key). This is evolution code, not engine, but it needs its own test. Declare it.

3. **(High) A null needs a declared "selection visibility" reading.** The calibration table shows the landscape is flat up to w ≈ 0.25 (34/30 at 0.05, 0.1 and 0.25, the same two pairs tipped) and rises only at 0.5 (0.59) and 1 (0.66). If the per-generation share noise at W exceeds those gains, selection cannot rank a w = 0.5 strain above X0 and a null says nothing about evolution. Fix: the pilot plays the comparator-origin controls at 0.5, 1 and 2, and E-G, on 8 independent draws of W mazes for W ∈ {16, 32, 64}, and reports the probability that each control out-ranks E-G on a draw. The pre-registration fixes W so that the w = 1 control out-ranks X0 on at least 90% of draws (or declares that no affordable W does, which ends the design). The null's wording then adds: "with a gradient the GA could see from w = 1".

4. **(High) The slot-factor rule measures the wrong thing.** "Children keep X0's chance level" is satisfied at every factor: right- and wrong-signed links are symmetric across children, so the children's mean share stays near 0.5 until B breaks. Fix: the rule is the largest candidate factor at which generation-1 children's entry rate is ≥ 0.95 of X0's (over all trials); report beside it the SD of the children's shares (the selectable variance). Candidates: 0.25, 1, 2, 4. Add 2: the stage A ladder dropped steeply between 1 and 4, and at factor 0.25 pure drift reaches |w| = 0.5 only after about 600 generations, so 0.25 is a null by construction unless the pilot shows otherwise.

5. **(Medium) The margin's arithmetic is wrong and its test is undeclared.** The control's gain over chance is 0.33, so half is 0.165, not 0.1. Fix: declare the margin as 0.15 (about half the control's gain), and the state "found the transfer" as the sign-flip test on per-run (share − 0.5) at 0.05 **and** the one-sided 95% t lower bound on the per-run gain ≥ margin. "Found a weak use" is the test alone. The share compared is over all trials; the entries-only share is reported beside.

6. **(Medium) The sign-flip p is misquoted.** For a one-sided exact sign-flip test the smallest p is 1/2^R, not 2/2^R. R = 5 already allows 0.031. R ≥ 7 is fine, but state the right number; with the budget I estimate R = 8 per arm is affordable (a trial costs about 1/24 of a main-task world, so a generation of 8 runs at W = 64 is roughly 2-3 minutes).

7. **(Medium) Play E-G on the validation and test blocks explicitly.** "Minus 0.5" assumes X0 enters every test trial. It entered 128/128 on the evaluation block but that is not guaranteed on a fresh block, and over all trials X0's share is 0.5 × entry rate. Fix: the per-run difference is champion share minus E-G's share on the same block (cheap, one strain), and the per-generation integrity check asserts real-first == decoy-first for every strain whose 32 slots are all zero (generation 0 in L, every generation in both arms where a strain has no links).

8. **(Medium) The champion rule needs its ties declared.** "The final population's best on a validation block" will have ties at identical shares. Fix: ties go to the higher final-generation fitness, then the smaller cross-link norm (the simpler genome), then the lowest index. Also note the whole final population must be played on the validation block, which `evolve_batch` does not do (it validates only each generation's best by fitness), so this is new code with a test.

9. **(Medium) The trial rollout must return zero progress.** `evolve_batch` computes count + c × progress. The new trial rollout returns a share as `score`; its `progress` must be zeros and the runs' shaping must be 0, asserted, or the shaping term silently enters fitness.

10. **(Low) F is worth running at reduced size, conditional on the pilot.** F is interpretable because any genome with links at 0 is structurally invariant, so F's cut-links control is still exact chance. But its inherited mutations cost 7% in the shuttle and the bias factor 0.02 is unconfirmed. Recommendation: run F in the pilot; keep it in the formal run at R = 4 only if its generation-1 entry rate stays ≥ 0.9 of X0's, otherwise drop it.

11. **(Low) Shared mazes across strains are fine; say why.** Common mazes give within-generation ranking under common random numbers, which reduces ranking noise. The shared test block across runs correlates the per-run differences, but the sign-flip test's null (no run's champion carries the cue) is still exact; the inference generalises to the 64 test mazes and should say so.

12. **(Low) Add the pilot's remaining measurements.** The share of pairs tipped per child at each factor, the children's entry rate per factor, seconds per generation and GPU memory at W ∈ {16, 32, 64} for 8 runs batched, and the neutral-walk reach at the chosen factor over the planned G.

**Owner's direction choices**

- **Whether a null in L ends E4-D.** The design's own reading is "this GA at this budget did not find it". The natural next arm, L seeded at the control's wiring at w = 0.5, separates "cannot discover" from "cannot climb". Whether that is in E4's scope and budget is the owner's call, and it should be decided before the formal run so it is not chosen after seeing L's result.
- **The margin value** (0.15 or another) is a scientific threshold, not a statistical one.
- **F's inclusion and R**, since they halve or double the formal run's cost within the 25.8 GPU-hours.
