**Verdict: go to v2 with these changes.** L answers a useful, narrow question. The scoring, tie handling and statistical decision rule need fixing before registration.

1. **Major — L is fair; the claimed gradient is unproved (§7.1).** L tests whether this GA recruits transfer through 32 available links between already engineered modules. It does not test discovery of communication generally. “A fitness gradient on every trial” is wrong: first-entry fitness is discontinuous and can remain flat across many mutations. A wrong-signed link can also prevent entry, not merely redirect it. The calibration gives 0.53125 at small comparator weights and 0.65625 at weight 1; it establishes neither a flat region nor a required weight of 2–3. **Fix:** describe accessibility as the unknown, and keep the engineered-cue qualification in the outcome wording.

2. **Critical — Fix the denominator everywhere (§7.2).** Use **fitness = real-first count / \(2W\)**; decoy and neither both score zero. X0’s all-trial score is **half its entry rate**, not necessarily 0.5. Nevertheless, 0.5 is a valid upper bound for any assignment-blind policy under paired assignments. Thus exceeding it establishes useful assignment dependence. Do not equate this contrast with intact-minus-cut unless cut enters every trial. The existing [evaluation record](D:/Claude/random/wormWars/experiments/E4-two-minds/E4-D/evaluate.json:182) already demonstrates the distinction: fixed-cue performance is 0.4921875. Report all three outcome counts.

3. **Major — No biological tie-breaker; randomize exact fitness ties (§7.2).** Entry rate is not a measure of the cue’s effect, and was already saturated for X0 on the feasibility blocks. Distance, neural margins or speed would introduce additional selection objectives. **Recommendation:** raw fitness only; uniformly random ordering within exact ties, using a recorded, separate per-run RNG stream. Apply this to elites and the parent pool. Current [`breed`](D:/Claude/random/wormWars/wormwars/evo/evolve.py:77) uses index-dependent `argsort`, which is unsuitable when most strains tie. Update lineage replay consistently; record tie rates and neutral lineage turnover.

4. **Major — Choose mutation strength by search performance, not generation-1 preservation (§7.3).** A population average near 0.5 can conceal beneficial and harmful children; keeping that average near chance does not demonstrate searchability. **Recommendation:** retain factors **{0.25, 1, 4}**, hence σ **{0.02, 0.08, 0.32}**. Pilot L at each factor, four runs each, 100 scored generations. Select each final population’s champion on pilot validation, evaluate it on a separate pilot assessment block, and choose the factor with the highest median assessment score; exact ties favor the smaller factor. Require integrity, but impose no generation-1 chance-preservation threshold. Publish every factor’s results. This is exploratory algorithm tuning, followed by fresh formal runs.

5. **Critical — The proposed “exact” primary test lacks its required null (§7.4).** [`sign_flip`](D:/Claude/random/wormWars/wormwars/e4/stats.py:32) is two-sided and exact under sign symmetry, not simply under a zero mean. Selected champions can produce asymmetric run distributions. Also, the smallest **one-sided** p-value is \(1/2^R\), not \(2/2^R\), absent zeros. **Recommendation:** retain the mean-performance estimand, use a **one-sided t procedure at 0.05**, explicitly approximate, and target **R = 16** pending pilot power/sensitivity checks. Use independent test blocks across runs. Do not justify replication by attainable p-value alone.

6. **Major — Require uncertainty to clear the practical margin (§7.4).** Choose **δ = 0.10**, meaning mean success above 0.60. It is a practical threshold, not half the control’s observed gain: half of 0.328125 is 0.1640625. Let \(L,U\) be one-sided 95% bounds for the mean gain above 0.5:
   - \(L>0.10\): practically relevant transfer found.
   - \(L>0,\ U<0.10\): weak transfer found.
   - \(L>0\), margin unresolved: transfer detected; practical size unresolved.
   - \(L\le0,\ U<0.10\): practical gain excluded; transfer not detected.
   - Otherwise: inconclusive.
   
   Integrity failure makes the result not evaluable. Predefine infrastructure recovery and algorithmic-failure handling; do not silently replace unsuccessful seeds. A significant test plus a point estimate above δ is insufficient.
