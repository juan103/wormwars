Findings:

1. **ROADMAP.md, 25 October entry, and README row: "Every run's plan was fixed and reviewed by Astra and Fable before it ran" / "each planned and reviewed by both before it ran".** Not true for M4c. Its census stage (the classification of eight fresh panels) started during the plan's review round 1, as M4C-RESULTS ("The run") and D361 ("Disclosed") both say; only the sets and arms ran after agreement. Fix: "before its arms ran (M4c's classification census started during the plan's first review round, D361)".

2. **ROADMAP.md, the 20 October annotation: "misleading ... only the median genome is ordinary".** Two problems. (a) M1's correction (M1-RESULTS "Correction to the memory roadmap's finding 1") says the "typical brain" half of the claim *stands* for the median genome and only the "what is extreme is the weighted share" half is misleading; the annotation tags the whole line. (b) "Only the median genome is ordinary" implies everything else is extreme, which M1 does not support: trimming the top 5% of contributors brings N2 inside each ensemble's range, and which genomes carry the lead ranked by retention "has not been measured (Astra)". Fix: "the 'amplitude-weighted share' half is misleading: N2's unweighted mean per-genome retention is extreme too and the weighting lowers its share; 'not in the typical genome' still holds for the median genome, while the upper quartile is extreme and the largest contributors carry much of the lead."

3. **README row: the M4 summary jumps from M2 to M4c's positive reading.** M4a and M4b, both "unresolved", are omitted, so a main reader sees one positive and one unresolved reading out of four M4 slices. Fix: one clause before the RIA sentence, e.g. "Cuts of the whole head core (M4a) and of each switch's own neurons (M4b) read unresolved; in N2, cutting RIA's ...".

4. **README row: "neither read-out alignment nor response amplitude explains the lead".** D1's registered wording is "amplitude does not account for most of N2's tick-30 lead; at matched amplitude more than a quarter of it remains", on this probe, with the mismatched-k effect unquantified. Same in ROADMAP's M1 bullet, "the lead survives whole at matched response amplitude" drops the results file's "within the used strata" qualifier (M1-RESULTS, amendment 1, "What f is here"). Fix: "amplitude does not account for most of the lead (f = 1.01 and 1.03 at matched amplitude)" in both places.

5. **ROADMAP M4d bullet and README row: "in all ten fresh host graphs (from 0.04% to 0.62%)".** The two percentages are pooled over 10,240 genomes per arm, not a per-host range (per host, T runs 4 to 10 of 1024). The source uses the same construction, but a reader of main will take it as per-host. Fix: "(pooled: 0.04% to 0.62%)".

6. **DECISIONS D365: "Every number is taken from the results files' 'In short' sections".** M1-RESULTS has no "In short" section; its numbers come from the readings table, Part A and the amendment, and the 0.586 line comes via the memory roadmap's annotation. Fix: "from the results files and the memory roadmap's annotations".

7. **ROADMAP and README "Next": "what a dense block carries (its size, weights and degrees)".** D364 and M4D-RESULTS word the option as "density and degrees". The gloss is defensible from the data (D − I and R − D) but it is Claude's, not the file's. Fix: use "density and degrees" or mark the expansion as a paraphrase.
