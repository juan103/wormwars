**Revise.** Closing M3 is sound with narrower wording. This is a useful first M4 slice, but it cannot support the proposed binary store/access verdict.

1. **Failure to show specificity does not establish access.** A null result could reflect insufficient duration, redundant maintenance, or poor control overlap. Report “maintenance specificity not demonstrated.” A positive result establishes that C3’s connections contribute to maintaining the history difference; it does not locate storage exclusively inside C3. The [roadmap’s decision rule](/D:/Claude/random/wormWars/docs/memory/MEMORY-ROADMAP.md:175) needs this qualification.

2. **Separate maintenance from settling, and trajectory convergence from attractor loss.** Ticks 1–20 immediately after the ramp can affect basin selection or settling. Add one delayed intervention on the separate-states genomes, preferably starting from their validated roots. After restoring the intact network, its original roots still exist: both histories reaching one root means erased basin identity, not “losing the switch.” Read-outs through tick 300 alone cannot establish common-root convergence; use targeted continuation for that claim.

3. **Edge count plus summed |weight| is insufficient matching.** Signed cancellation, source activity, saturation and target placement determine the actual perturbation. Predefine control draws, tolerances and matching on realized genome weights; assess the first affected update’s perturbation to full-state separation and output, plus displacement of the histories’ mean state. Merely reporting unequal immediate effects does not control them. Where comparable controls cannot be found, declare the comparison unresolved. Never select controls using later retention.

4. **Define “read-only” operationally.** The [read-out](/D:/Claude/random/wormWars/wormwars/exp03/measures.py:82) depends directly on `tanh(v)`, so cutting edges without advancing the state has zero effect. Cutting for one tick changes dynamics, and comparing that with twenty hold ticks confounds timing with duration. Specify exposure and recovery explicitly; “hold beats read-only” is not sufficient evidence.

5. **Close M3 as a completed screen, not a universal negative.** Say “no strong within-ensemble association for the tested predictors.” The [anatomy script](/D:/Claude/random/wormWars/scripts/memory_m1/anatomy.py:15) did **not** include C3-kept-fraction in its correlation predictors; that proposed analysis is new. Keep reference and selected graphs separate, acknowledge six reference graphs per ensemble, and distinguish C3 from batch 7’s result-defined core.

Keep intact classes, eligibility and denominators fixed across intervention comparisons. The proposed deferrals are reasonable; benchmark the actual arm count before committing to one–three hours.
