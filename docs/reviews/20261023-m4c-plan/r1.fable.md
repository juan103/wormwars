**Verdict: revise** (one design point and two small code points; nothing that invalidates the run).

I read the plan, `m4c.py`, `m4c_read.py`, the tests and sabotages, and checked them against `m4a.py`, `m4b.py` and `census.py`. The code does what the plan says: the X and W draws, the stars, the dissection arms, the 2% and 5% tolerances, the C1 skip (restored in `finally`), the 60-genome rule, the two-way bootstrap and the reading's boundaries all match the text. The smoke's 125 arms are the expected count.

Findings, ranked:

1. **The reading is W-driven in practice, and W is biased in opposite directions for the two branches.** X will almost surely be beaten: M4a and M4b already showed that cuts inside C3 erase far more than exterior cuts, so lower(D_X) > 0.10 reflects C3 membership, not RIA. Everything then rests on W, which holds 67 to 99 edges spread over up to 28 neurons. That favours W, so the positive branch is conservative, as the plan says. But the "matched" branch fires on `any` family, so upper(D_W) < 0.10 alone yields "RIA's prominence is what its weight predicts". A W match can equally mean "cutting a third of the non-RIA core erases as often", which is coverage, not weight; the dissection compared stars of similar count, not scattered sets. Cheap fix: add a same-count core family (20 random sets of 26 or 29 non-RIA C3 edges per side, about 40 arms, roughly 25 CPU minutes) and read "matched by weight" only when W matches and the same-count sets erase clearly less. Otherwise word that outcome "matched inside C3 at more edges, or by coverage". Also make the reading record which family triggered it.

2. **Panel validity is not checked.** The census records `valid` (M1's rule) and M2's plan dropped invalid panels, but `switch_ids` reads classes without looking at it. Low risk for N2 (937 to 1010 eligible per panel in M2), one line to add.

3. **Free consistency check unused.** The side arm RIAR and the dissection arm "C3:RIAR" cut identical edges and both run; the reading should assert their outcomes agree rather than silently duplicate.

4. **The Spearman 0.50 is over overlapping stars.** Each C3 edge sits in two neurons' stars, and RIA's edges are inside the SMD and RMD stars that rank next. Say so where the correlation is quoted; it partly restates RIA.

5. **`runs/memory-m4c/census.log` already exists** (empty) in the real output folder. If the census started before review closed, the plan should say so. Harmless for the arms, but "fixed here before anything runs" should be accurate.

6. **Power note, not a change.** With about 110 genomes the D intervals will be roughly 0.08 to 0.10 wide on each side, so a positive needs D near 0.2 or more on both sides. The smoke's gaps (0.5 to 0.75 against 0.2 to 0.33, four genomes) leave room, but say the threshold in the plan.
