I've now read both rounds, the 03mc results, every spike script and output, and the engine code the numbers depend on. Two code facts first, then the verdicts.

**Two things checked in the code that affect how the spikes read.** The T1 task config sets 32 substeps, so batch 1's "16 / 32" arm compared 16 against the baseline. The conclusion holds, but the baseline was already 32. More important: every spike rewiring keeps all 38 of N2's autapses fixed and carries each weight with its source slot, while 03's samplers permute weights freely over the new edges and let autapses move. So the "free rewiring" arms are not SH graphs. Their higher retention than batch 1's SH graphs may be autapses and source-attached weights, not the kept 27% of edges. Comparisons inside batch 7 are fair. Comparisons between any batch-7 arm and 03mc's ensembles are not.

## The leading reading, prosecuted

**The description is wrong for the median genome.** The per-genome state-norm ratio is identical across graphs (0.197 vs 0.192 and 0.200). The median read-out retention is at the 65th to 85th percentile of the nulls. "Decays more slowly" is true of N2's upper quartile and false of its lower half. The honest sentence is: N2 writes a larger, more concentrated difference, its upper quartile of genomes keeps more of it, and one genome in twenty is locally unstable at the operating point.

**The core is selected on the dependent variable.** It is the 30 neurons with the largest start difference. That the read-out difference survives when those neurons' wiring is kept is partly tautology. The non-tautological content is exactly two comparisons: hubs frozen instead of core, and core dissolved against periphery scrambled. Three graphs each. The second is a 0.09 gap with ranges that nearly touch.

**Storage and delivery are not separated.** The core contains the write path (AWC and AWA to AIY to AIZ to AIB to RIA and RIM) and the read-out. A strong feed-forward pipe into saturated motor neurons that then leak at their own tau would give the same core-frozen result. The regrowth to 83% after erasing the turn 8 shows the interneuron half re-delivers within 30 ticks, but a tau-20 neuron re-delivers for 30 ticks too. Nothing yet shows the core retains more than its neurons' leak, except the 5% unstable draws.

**Amplitude is untested.** N2's response is five times a null's. Every retention number is a ratio, but the dynamics are nonlinear. Scale the ramp-end difference by 0.25, 0.5 and 2 with the midpoint fixed. If N2's retention falls toward the nulls' at small amplitude, the lead is a consequence of the response size that 03mc already explained, and the structural question collapses into Q-B and Q-C.

**Erasure arms conflate location with amount.** Erasing the top 30 by start difference removes most of the state difference. A low share afterwards says where the difference was at tick 0, not where it was kept. The arms need to report the erased fraction of the start norm, or compare with the linear forecast of the same erasure. One random 30 on seed 7400 dropped the share to 0.311, as much as erasing the turn neurons.

**A null graph can do it.** SH-class-2030000 reaches 0.442 and 0.400 on the two panels, with a median above N2's and its residual on RIAL. The 640 graphs and their arrays contain two to five such graphs per ensemble. Nobody has looked at what they share.

**The measure.** The registered share is an amplitude-weighted mean of per-genome retention, and N2's weights are heavy-tailed. The question is well posed only once split. I would register three quantities: the median per-genome retention among genomes whose start difference is at least a tenth of the graph's mean, which measures typical slowness; the fraction whose full-state difference grows over the first ten ticks, which measures instability; and the registered ratio of means, for continuity. The evidence so far predicts the transplant moves the second and third, not the first.

## Roadmap, and one crazy idea

1. **Settle the measure and the amplitude.** CPU, two hours, exploratory. Four panels of 1024 on N2 and eight nulls, including the top-retention null from each ensemble, reporting all three quantities above with panel SEs. Add the alpha-scaling arm on N2 and two nulls. If retention at alpha 0.25 reaches the null level, the plan changes: the question becomes response size, and steps 4 and 5 are dropped.
2. **Jacobian census on batch 7's graphs.** CPU, one hour, exploratory. Rho-above-1 fraction and lin30 for core-frozen, hub-frozen, dissolved and scrambled, plus the 30-by-30 core block's own radius. If hub-frozen graphs also have 5% unstable draws, instability is generic to dense subgraphs and the story is write-read alignment, family 9. If only core-frozen keeps it, the core's wiring is special.
3. **A predictor that does not use N2's residual.** CPU, minutes, exploratory. For each of the 640 graphs: density of the subgraph induced by the turn read-out's two-step in-neighbourhood, and a walk-count write-read product. Correlate within ensembles with q75 and the growth fraction. Also the three cheap kills: complement erasure, the gap dissipation ratio, and the antisymmetric share. If the predictor's within-ensemble rho is under 0.2, the core reading is an N2 fact with no structural handle and the transplant should be deferred.
4. **Dissect the core.** CPU, two hours, exploratory. Core-frozen shuffles with, in turn: core-internal edges rewired within the core at fixed degrees; core-internal weights permuted; reciprocal pairs broken; only the sensory half frozen; only the motor half frozen. Run with 03's sampler conventions as well as the spikes', so the arms map onto 03mc's ensembles. The arm that costs retention names what to transplant.
5. **The transplant, pre-registered.** GPU, needs the owner's word, roughly two hours. Insert the surviving core structure from step 4 into 32 fresh random graphs of a different degree sequence, with the hubs' 202 edges as the matched control inserted the same way. Fix in advance: share30 rises from about 0.25 to at least 0.40, growth fraction from under 1% to at least 3%, hub control moves neither, and which of the three measures is primary. The core-frozen shuffle is already a half-transplant that worked, so the owner's idea has earned a registered test. A failure in a foreign host would mean the core needs N2's degree sequence around it.
6. **Only then the behavioural question**, on the roadmap's existing memory-task track.

**The crazy idea the evidence favours.** The memory is an instability of the head motor circuit. RIA, RMD and SMD are the real worm's head-bending oscillator, and an oscillator sits at the edge of stability by design. Under random signs that circuit crosses the edge one draw in twenty, and those draws are the brains that remember. If so, the leading eigenvalue of the unstable genomes should be complex more often than chance and its eigenvector should sit on RMD and SMD. Batch 3's Jacobians answer both in seconds. The reversed graph's sign flip in the amplitude-retention correlation, from minus 0.2 to plus 0.5, is the kind of oddity that reading predicts and no other family does.
