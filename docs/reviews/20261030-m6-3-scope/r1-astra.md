The following revisions matter.

1. **Give fading history effects equal standing with switches.** M6-0 reports 101 of 102 champions as fading on the primary bank. It also distinguishes larger absolute effects from shorter persistence relative to each genome’s initial effect. Preserve that distinction in the biology comparison. A literature review of bistability alone would miss the principal evolved result. [M6-0 results](D:/Claude/random/wormWars/docs/memory/M6-0-RESULTS.md)

2. **Separate four evidential claims:** history changes neural activity; history changes behavior; activity persists through a delay; a particular mechanism maintains information needed for later behavior. None automatically establishes the next. Likewise, bimodality, regenerative responses, prolonged calcium and two stable equilibria are different observations. Strong evidence for bistability would include history-dependent occupancy under the same maintained conditions, switching or resetting, and stability tests.

3. **Make sensory adaptation and short-lived cellular or synaptic processes explicitly admissible.** Excluding associative learning is reasonable. Excluding a mechanism because it resembles short-term plasticity would prejudge where the worm’s history dependence comes from. The model’s omissions are reasons to identify alternative mechanisms.

4. **Retain the exception for distributed dynamics, and apply it substantively.** Dunn et al. directly connect sensory history to later turning through distributed head-related oscillations. This belongs in scope even though it uses whole-brain imaging. Head oscillation can be a candidate memory mechanism, as well as a confound. **[READ]** [Dunn et al., 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12829590/)

5. **Correct the anatomical shorthand.** C3 is the top 30 neurons by internal degree **within the turn read-out’s one-step chemical input neighborhood**, not the globally highest-degree 30 neurons or an independently established biological module. The model’s RIA result concerns vulnerability to a temporary connection cut; the synthesis explicitly says storage is unshown. [M3 definition](D:/Claude/random/wormWars/docs/memory/M3-NOTE.md:14), [synthesis](D:/Claude/random/wormWars/docs/memory/SYNTHESIS.md)

6. **Reduce peripheral material.** Move 03h’s ASK result to an implementation note. It does not constrain biological ASK memory. Keep roaming/dwelling studies as a small set of mechanistic comparators, and avoid expanding into a general survey of learning, global states or every neuromodulator.

The main pitfalls are these:

- **Equating model input with sensory experience.** The default interface sends the same positive concentration signal to AWA, AWC and ASE pairs. Its comments explicitly call this a modeling choice. Matching external odor concentration also does not guarantee matched internal sensory drive after different histories. [Interface](D:/Claude/random/wormWars/configs/interface.yaml:19)

- **Equating a rate variable, membrane voltage and fluorescence.** The principal issue is what the state variable represents and which dynamics it omits. Calcium kinetics and indicator filtering can prolong a trace. RIA’s functional signals occupy compartments that a single scalar per neuron cannot represent. **[READ]** [Hendricks](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3393794/)

- **Confusing axes and anatomical units.** RIAL/RIAR are left/right neurons; nrD/nrV are dorsal/ventral regions within their axons. The model’s dorsal/ventral motor groups are mapped onto planar turning. Record exact neuron identities, compartments and behavioral measurements before calling anything a match.

- **Calling an intervention a storage assay.** Erasing a distinction can disrupt input, maintenance, state transitions, read-out or movement. The model’s temporary RIA-edge cut leaves the original stable states available after restoration. It is also different from biological ablation or silencing. [Synthesis](D:/Claude/random/wormWars/docs/memory/SYNTHESIS.md)

- **Mistaking oscillatory phase for sustained activity—or discarding it as noise.** Phase-dependent memory is a distinct candidate. Dunn’s results also leave open additional molecular or neuronal carriers, so the literature report should distinguish the proposed mechanism from everything experimentally identified. **[READ]** [Dunn](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12829590/)

- **Converting neuronal influence into behavioral equivalence.** Head-bend amplitude, trajectory curvature, reversal probability, turn direction and roaming/dwelling are separate outputs. A paper about one cannot silently support a claim about another.

- **Treating random parameter draws as biological populations.** “Fewer than 2% of genomes” measures this sampling distribution. The code initializes chemical signs randomly; it does not identify physiological signs or fit individual worms. Likewise, evolved performance has not established that the measured probe effect is behaviorally used. [Initialization](D:/Claude/random/wormWars/wormwars/brain.py:213)

- **Turning a bounded search into an absence claim.** “No direct demonstration found in the sources checked” is defensible. “The worm has no such mechanism” is not. Record search coverage and failed access separately from negative experimental findings.

For the report’s evidence table, I would require: exact claim, source/version, reading level, preparation, neuron or compartment, stimulus history, matched final conditions, measured variable, observation duration, intervention, and what remains unidentified. That would make the second-round review concrete without expanding this into another research program.
