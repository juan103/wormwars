## Readings and gaps

- **In short 1 and §1's reading overstate "intrinsic".** The intrinsic mechanism is a fitted single-compartment model whose authors say "other parameter sets could be identified" (`quotes-structure-models.txt:102`), and the report's own Open column records L&G's question whether the plateau is partly synaptic. Say "modelled as intrinsic; whether partly synaptic is open". Also say plainly that the RMD plateau rests on one primary report, read here through its abstract and two commentaries, in a dissected preparation, with no replication found. "(dissected)" is an inference; the letter says only "in situ".
- **In short 2's heading** states "the memory is phase" as fact. The body correctly says "the authors find", and the table lists "other intermediate carriers" as open. Make the heading match.
- **Later RMD electrophysiology: none found, and the report should say it searched.** A bounded search turns up only models: Nicoletti's 2020 RMD model [5], and Nicoletti 2024, which models RIM, AVA and AIY and speaks of "bistable responses" among measured neurons [6]. RIM is in C3, so that survey's classification of RIM belongs in §1 at abstract level, and Nicoletti's `:98` cites it for neurons "classified as bistable" that fully repolarise. No later RMD recording surfaced.
- **The SMD lead from round 1 was not followed, and it exists.** Cecere et al. and Rusu et al. (bioRxiv 2021) report whole-brain imaging of restrained worms under bacterial stimuli, with SMDD and SMDV clusters responding tens of seconds after onset or removal, and network states in which motor clusters are or are not receptive [10][11][12]. Unreviewed, abstract level, but it is the nearest thing in the literature to "food history sets an SMD state" and should be named in §2 or under Not checked. The Li preprint abstract also says RMD subgroups show calcium dynamics correlated with "slow transitions between behavior states" [13], a slow state signal in a read-out neuron, missing from the Li row.
- **Atanas 2023 was read but not used.** Seventeen quotes were gathered; none is in the report. It shows interneurons integrating recent velocity over tens of seconds, RIC among them and RIC is in C3 (`quotes-states-history.txt:119`, `:123`, `:132`), a decoder reading past velocity at 20 s (`:121`), but current activity failing to predict future head bending (`:134`), and RMDD's encoding flipping with movement direction (`:125`). That is history held as integrated levels, of motor history not food, plus a negative on the read-out. It belongs in §2.

## Closing section, recommendation and verdict

The neutral framing is right for a literature report; the recommendation belongs in the cover note to the owner, as the options note already does. My recommendation to the owner is **option 4, stop, and fold this check into the synthesis as its biology section.** Both named directions add a mechanism the model lacks, and neither has a worm-side target the model could be scored against: the RMD plateau's function is a hypothesis about which "almost nothing is known", and the phase memory is one study in paralysed worms with its carriers open. The thread's deliverable is complete as facts about rate networks on this wiring. If the owner wants one cheap thing first, run the already-classified switch genomes with the food returned to baseline after the ramp, CPU minutes, to learn whether their states outlast the input. That settles whether the switch differs in kind from Kunert-Graf's multistability. It is a description, not a new question.
