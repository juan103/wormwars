**Verdict: revise.** The question is the right one and the relief signal is the right instrument, but the design as written would not test what §1 claims. Two of the problems are structural (a missing control and an unmatched routing path), one is statistical (the fitness variable is almost binary at generation 0 on 8 worlds), and one is a gap in A's logic (no registered test that the link exists at all under level). None is a wording fix.

## 1. Does it test what it claims?

**Finding 1 (A, high): generation-0 fitness on Task N is zero-inflated, and 8 worlds make it nearly binary.** The repository's own numbers: 74% of 256 random N2 genomes scored zero on every one of 64 pilot worlds, mean 0.031 targets per episode, best genome 0.69 (`experiments/E1-navigation/RESULTS.md:70-72`); 04a's generation-0 populations scored zero on every training world for 78-97% of genomes (`experiments/04a-navigation-primitive/RESULTS.md:174`). On 8 worlds the expected count per genome is about 0.25, so most of the 1 024 genomes tie at 0 and most of the rest have exactly one arrival. A Spearman ρ over that is a point-biserial on "scored at all" plus noise, and a difference of two such ρ's with a 0.05 smallest effect is not interpretable. B's top-decile mean becomes a count of lucky single arrivals. The repository already acted on this once: 04a registered a shaping term because counts were mostly zero at generation 0. Fix: either many more worlds per genome (32 or 64, trading genomes for worlds: 512 × 32 costs twice the rollouts of 1 024 × 8), or a registered graded secondary score (04a's shaping), or both. Shared worlds also make the D_g's correlated across graphs, so the graph-level test's SE is understated with 8 worlds.

**Finding 2 (A, high): no uninformative-bit control, so D_g > 0 is expected under the null.** Under relief, each genome's fitness gains a new component: how its random weights happen to route ASK to the motors. That component is independent of the history probe, so it adds variance to fitness and attenuates ρ_R mechanically. D = ρ_L − ρ_R would be positive even if history mattered equally in both conditions. Astra's scoping answer asked for "matched sham conditions" for exactly this reason. Fix: a third condition, **sham**, in which ASK receives a bit with the same marginal rate but no relation to the level (a registered Bernoulli rate, measured on null graphs in a pilot). A's registered contrast becomes ρ_sham − ρ_relief; D against level is reported descriptively. A delayed bit is not a good sham: falls persist for many ticks at σ = 6, so a lagged bit is still informative.

**Finding 3 (B, high): the bit's routing is unmatched in every ensemble, so B restates 02's direct-edge story.** `constrained_neurons` in `wormwars/exp03/samplers.py:53-56` caps only the six food pairs of M0, R1 and R2. ASKL/R are not in it, so SH-route and SH-mirror leave ASK's direct read-out edges free. 02 found shuffles give food neurons direct read-out weights of 1.5 to 43 against N2's near zero (`experiments/02-screening/RESULTS.md:257-263`); the same will happen to ASK. A shuffle with ASK wired straight onto AVB or SMD exploits the bit more than N2 can, its U is more negative, and N2 ranks high on U for a reason that has nothing to do with memory. U_g as designed cannot tell "N2 needs memory more" from "N2's ASK is further from the motors". Fix: extend the routing cap to the receiving pair for this experiment (a parameter on `constrained_neurons`, registered as a new ensemble name, with a test), and report each graph's ASK direct read-out count and weight (`direct_readout`, `samplers.py:65`) as a covariate. The sham arm bounds the "added drive" part but not this part.

**Finding 4 (A, medium): no registered test that the link exists under level.** D > 0 is read as "history helps when memory is needed", but that presumes ρ_L > 0. A positive D with ρ_L at zero and ρ_R negative would pass. Register A1: mean ρ_L over null graphs > 0, plus N2's ρ_L with its genome bootstrap; A2: the relief dependence (Finding 2). Report per ensemble, with N2 separate, not one mean over "129 graphs".

**Finding 5 (A, medium): |history| is direction-blind, and the hypothesis has a direction.** KTM's memory acts on the turn only: turn when the level fell (`wormwars/e03a/probe.py:96-97`). What helps a random brain is turning more after a fall than after a rise. The probe's rising − falling difference on the signed raw turn is large for a genome that turns left after a rise and right after a fall, which is useless. The zero floor makes |history| roughly monotone with fitness anyway (wrong-direction genomes cannot score below 0), but the direction-specific predictor is strictly more sensitive: |motor turn after falling| − |motor turn after rising| at the final tick. `history()` returns only differences (`wormwars/exp03/measures.py:118-121`), so this needs a small extension returning per-history read-outs, with a test; not an engine change. Register the directional turn predictor as primary and |history| on the raw turn (P4's numerator) as the capacity secondary; forward descriptive.

**Finding 6 (both, medium): no manipulation check on neural brains.** The go condition is on scripted families only. If random brains do not exploit the bit at all, ρ_R ≈ ρ_L and B's U ≈ 0 for every graph, and a null is uninformative. Register, as a reported check: the within-graph shift of the top decile under relief against sham. Astra's point about E2d applies at generation 0 too.

**Finding 7 (low): multiplicity and the gate.** A and B are two primaries and C runs on "A or B". Either state the gate's family-wise error openly or apply Holm to the claims. The B verdict per ensemble should follow 03's intersection-union wording (`experiments/03-generation0/RESULTS.md:63-65`).

**Finding 8 (low): the go condition under relief is partly an identity.** KT reading the bit *is* KTM (same comparison, first tick matched). The relief check therefore tests whether KTS+bit beats KTM by more than 0.3, which is the right question about P4's longer timescale (Astra's point), but say so.

I found no leak of data into the registered decisions. Two places need explicit disjointness: the 8 fitness worlds against the tuning and check worlds, and the calibration genomes against the fitness genomes (not a leak, but say which).

## 2. The open questions

- **Receiving neurons:** ASKL/R, with conditions. They are silent in Task N (`wormwars/e1/task.py:34`), bilateral, sensory, outside the read-out, and the interface sums channels per neuron, so a second channel on ASK works. AWC is wrong: it mixes the bit onto the level's own neuron, which Fable's scoping advice ruled out. A non-sensory pair gains nothing. The conditions are Findings 2 and 3, plus registering gain 1.0, the first-tick value, and that the bit is computed from the reading the brain receives (after any food probe, before gain and clamp). Note the interface hash changes, so this is a new registered interface variant, not "the pinned interface".
- **Ensembles and counts:** SH plus one route-constrained ensemble with the cap extended to ASK. Between SH-route and SH-mirror the difference is small for this question; take SH-mirror if the sampler settles in time (03mc noted it as the slowest), else SH-route. 64 each is the floor for B's rank rule (p ≥ 1/65). A needs far fewer graphs than worlds: if budget binds, cut graphs to 32 per ensemble before cutting worlds.
- **A's statistic:** partial Spearman of the directional turn predictor with fitness, controlling for the steady contrast, per graph; A1 and A2 as in Finding 4; ≥ 32 worlds. The smallest effect of interest cannot be set from existing files; nothing relates history to fitness yet. Set it from a registered pilot on 2-4 null graphs (03 did this), in fitness units as well as ρ (for example the top-against-bottom history quartile's difference in targets per episode).
- **B's statistic:** the top-decile mean as primary (about 100 genomes), the expected best of 32 by order statistics as secondary (it rests on the top 3% and is noisier). Report both components of U.
- **Frozen-champion check:** after, as its own small exploratory item, not in 03h. It is about evolved brains, needs new replay machinery and validation (Astra said so), and does not share 03h's population. Put it with Stage C's champion analysis if C runs.
- **Cap:** not 4 as written, once worlds and the sham are added. Rough arithmetic at 892 episodes/s: 128 null graphs × 3 conditions × 512 genomes × 32 worlds plus N2 at 1 024 × 32 is about 6.4 M episodes, about 2.0 h, plus 0.9 h calibration and 0.5 h probe, about 3.5 h. The 892 figure is 03a's "measure" stage (87 552 worlds in 98 s, `experiments/03a-self-consistency/pilot-dev/report.json:158-162`), a different batch composition from 1 024 strains × 8 worlds. Time one null graph before fixing the cap; 6 h with that margin is defensible.
