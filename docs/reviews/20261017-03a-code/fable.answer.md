## Verdict: ready for pilot-dev, with small fixes

I found no bug in the core implementation that would make pilot-dev's output wrong. The union mask, background copy, information boundary, replica exchange, strata, null, family 2, comparisons, panel rule, structural comparators and probe all match DESIGN.md v7.1 as I read it. The fixes below are about what pilot-dev measures and whether its budget survives, not about correctness. One of them (finding 1) costs no GPU time and should run before the owner's word, because its answer could change the search budget before 6 GPU-hours are spent.

## Findings

**1. The 100-generation concern is well founded, and a free CPU check bounds it (high; `wormwars/e03a/search.py:144-188`, `tests/test_e03a_search.py:202`).** With p_move = 1/3, each replica proposes about 2.7 partner moves per generation. A move helps only if it drops a non-partner and adds a partner, which from a random start has probability about (m − k)/(K − m) per move: for 10 partners among 301 candidates, about 3% per move, falling as partners are found. Under a *perfect* signal that is roughly 7 improving generations per 100 per replica, so Q stays well below 1 in 100 generations even before the 16-world integer count blurs the signal. The planted test uses K = 16; the plan admits K = 30 is already slow. Run the planted-optimum problem at K = 301 with the true counts of a real target (several seeds, the four tuning settings) on the CPU and record the Q trajectory. It is an upper bound on what the GPU search can do, costs nothing, and tells you now whether 100 generations can give detectable enrichment or whether the design's search budget needs amending first. Pilot-dev's own measurement (Q every 10 generations, the 2× search) is the right kind but is one noisy sample of four target-brains.

**2. The longer search reports score gain only, not what the question needs (medium; `scripts/e03a.py:596-598`).** `longer_gain` is the mean final count of 2 searches minus 5; its SE will be of order 0.15, so a threshold frozen from it is noise. The longer searches' Q trajectories and final AUCs are in their traces and results but are not written (`per_search` covers `free` labels only, line 573). Add the `longer` searches to `per_search` so Q at generation 100 and 200 within the same search, and the AUC at 200, are recorded. That is the direct measurement of "are 100 generations enough".

**3. Ranking agreement by Spearman on near-empty vectors will read as zero regardless (medium; `scripts/e03a.py:584-586`).** Each |weight| vector has about 25 nonzero entries of 903; Spearman between two such vectors is dominated by the shared zeros and will sit near 0 even when both searches found the same few true partners. A threshold frozen from it would be meaningless. Report the partner-set Jaccard per type beside it, with its expectation under independent random sets, and freeze the threshold on that.

**4. The pilot-dev budget has little margin, and a cap hit loses the whole running stage (medium; `scripts/e03a.py:390-407, 519-545`, `PILOT-DEV.md` §1).** The estimate of 5.4 h uses 420 episodes/s, measured on 8-run lockstep batches (2 048 worlds). The two evolution stages here run 2-run batches (512 worlds per rollout), which will be slower per world on the GPU; at 250/s they cost about 1.2 h instead of 0.7 h, putting the total near 5.9 h against a 6 h cap. `CapReached` inside `run_lockstep` discards every search in progress and `full` is the stage most likely to be hit. Two cheap mitigations: write a projection after `evolve-nip` from the recorded `batch_seconds` and reduce `tune` (for example round A to two ladders) if it does not fit, recording the change; and checkpoint `tune` per round and `full` per target-brain so a cap hit keeps what finished.

**5. The relevance bootstrap materialises about 5 GB (medium; `wormwars/e03a/panel.py:81`).** `drop.mean(axis=1)[:, wi]` is [neurons, 10 000, 512] float64: 5.3 GB for about 130 representatives, 10 GB on N2. The smoke test runs B = 200 and cannot see it. If RAM is short the panel stage fails after the GPU scan has been paid for. Compute the resampled means as a matrix product with a [B, worlds] count matrix, or loop over neurons.

**6. Rounds B and C are decided far below their noise (low, but the pre-registration must say so; `PILOT-DEV.md` §2).** The mean over 8 searches has a between-search SD likely 0.1 to 0.3 targets, so the 0.02 tie margin almost never binds and the choice is effectively "the first setting unless a lucky draw". That is acceptable for a frozen protocol, but the pre-registration should describe the settings as chosen by this rule, not as optimised.

**7. Diagnostic thresholds will come from two high-relevance targets only (low; `PILOT-DEV.md` §1, DESIGN.md §8 criterion 6).** Pilot-check applies them to a low target too, where turnover and agreement behave differently with no task signal. Either scope criterion 6 to the high targets or state in the pre-registration how the low target's diagnostics are read.

**8. Permutation seed is per target-brain here; it must be per target in the formal driver (low; `scripts/e03a.py:565-566`).** `null_seed + bi` gives each of the four target-brains its own permutations. The design's null is one draw applied to every brain and arm of a target. Fine for pilot-dev, but note it so the formal code seeds by target.

**9. NIP pinning on the intact mask: accept, but annotate the design (low; `wormwars/e03a/arms.py:36-43`).** I checked the equivalence claim: `Genome.mutate` draws the same noise with or without scales and multiplies X's edges by zero, `delete_neurons` zeroes the same edges, and the arms test runs a real lockstep evolution with a sabotage check. X's bias is not zeroed, which is harmless since X is isolated. This is a departure from §3's text, so under rule 2 it needs a dated annotation beside the design, not only a line in PILOT-DEV.md.

**10. Tests: two could pass while the property fails (low).** `test_e03a_reinsert.py:178` checks pinning on a spec built *without* X's edges, so `x_edges_zero` cannot fail there; the real pinning path is covered only by `test_e03a_arms.py:41`. Point the PILOT-DEV §5 row at the arms test. `test_e03a_probe.py:58` checks the first 2L actions are unperturbed and that something later differs, but not that the difference can start at 2L + 1; a probe resetting one period late would pass. Assert inequality within the first 3L actions on at least one of the four strains. The other tests check what they claim; the no-task test's tolerances (24 replicates, ≤ 4 of 24 below 0.05) are loose but adequate, and the surviving mutant is genuinely equivalent.

**11. Small reporting gaps (low).** `stage_report` falls back silently to 420/s for any missing category (`scripts/e03a.py:668-671`); record when a fallback was used. The interface and non-interface AUCs, precision at k and PR-AUC exist in `null.py` but are not written by `full`; add them so the frozen protocol can name them.
