# Review: the M4a plan (round 2) — a smoke run changes the design

Read-only, in the WormWars repository. Draft 2 of `docs/memory/M4A-PLAN.md` took all your round-1 points (archived in
`docs/reviews/20261022-m4a-plan/`). The code is written (`scripts/memory_m4/m4a.py`, `m4a_read.py`; tests
`tests/test_memory_m4a.py`, 6 pass; sabotages `scripts/memory_m4/sabotage_m4a.py`, `runs/m4a-sabotage.txt`, 7 of 7
fail as they must). A smoke run on 64 N2 genomes per seed (other draws than the census; nothing from the real panels)
found two design problems. Outputs in `runs/memory-m4a-smoke/`:
- `part1_smoke_log.txt`, `readings_smoke.json`: the plan's own pipeline. **Only 15 and 24 of about 60 eligible
  genomes matched.** For most genomes, cutting C3's 260 internal edges attenuates the 3-tick separation more than
  cutting **all 2696 external edges** can (median m* 0.16; every genome with m* above about 0.22 is unmatched). So the
  coverage rule fires on every arm, and worse, the **common matched subset selects the genomes on which the C3 cut has
  the least immediate effect**: on it, L ≈ 0 and the core does not collapse (c ≈ 0.75 to 0.88).
- `isolation.txt` (`scripts/memory_m4/smoke_isolation.py`), on all genomes: C3's internal cut for ticks 1 to 30
  collapses the core to 0.23 and 0.55 of intact and removes 0.64 and 0.32 of the separation at tick 60 (read-out 0.68,
  0.51); cutting every chemical input to C3 does about as much or more; cutting gap junctions touching C3 *raises* the
  separation.
- `count_matched.txt` (`scripts/memory_m4/smoke_count_matched.py`): the same against all external edges and three
  random sets of 260 external edges. Random count-matched cuts barely move the 3-tick separation (0.002 to 0.03,
  against C3's 0.18 to 0.29) but their **losses at tick 60 vary from −0.01 to 0.37 (read-out 0.04 to 0.62)** between
  draws, and losses often grow after restoration (tick 100 above tick 30). These brains are fragile to any 30-tick cut.

**Proposed draft 3** (please judge):
1. **Read over all eligible genomes,** never a matched subset (the selection above).
2. **Primary control: count-matched random external cuts,** 20 independent draws of |internal| edges with both ends
   outside C3, the same draws for every genome of a panel, plus the whole exterior as a descriptive bound. The reading
   asks whether C3's loss at the reading tick exceeds the 95th percentile of the 20 random cuts' losses by the 0.10
   margin, on both panels, with the bootstrap over genomes applied to C3 minus the random cuts' 95th percentile.
3. **The effect-matched control stays as a secondary,** reported with its coverage and explicitly flagged as biased
   toward weakly affected genomes; it is not read.
4. **Immediate attenuation reported as a covariate**: since C3's cut is far stronger immediately, a positive reading
   says "cutting C3's internal edges for 30 ticks costs more later than cutting as many edges elsewhere", which is a
   per-edge necessity claim, not "beyond equal immediate effect". The wording changes accordingly.
5. **Per class, descriptively:** the fragility suggests separate-states genomes switching basin; Part 2 (from the
   roots) stays as planned and speaks to that.
6. The collapse condition stays (C3's cut does collapse the core on all genomes in the smoke).
Cost: about 20 more 100-tick runs per N2 panel (about 40 minutes each); the reference comparison keeps the C3 cut and
5 random draws.
