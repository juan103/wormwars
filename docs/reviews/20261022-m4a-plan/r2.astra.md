**Revise.** Agree with using all eligible genomes, making count-matched cuts primary, and removing effect matching from the verdict. Five fixes, ranked:

1. **The bootstrap does not cover uncertainty over random cuts.** Twenty draws give a fragile 95th percentile, determined by the largest two losses under ordinary interpolation. Resampling genomes cannot capture uncertainty from sampling those masks. With this budget, retain 20 but explicitly condition the conclusion on these sampled masks. Within every paired genome resample, recompute each mask’s **panel-aggregate loss**, then their percentile and the contrast. Generalizing to the population’s 95th percentile requires more masks and uncertainty over masks.

2. **The claimed collapse is false under your threshold.** [isolation.txt](D:/Claude/random/wormWars/runs/memory-m4a-smoke/isolation.txt:7) gives **c = 0.546** for seed 7700, failing c ≤ 0.5. These are aggregate ratios, not evidence that every genome collapses. Keep the declared threshold, evaluate it on the actual eligible population, and accept “unresolved” when it fails. Preserve the actual-loss condition too.

3. **Use your operational wording; drop “per-edge necessity.”** This tests whether removing the entire C3 edge set causes greater persistent loss than sampled exterior sets of equal cardinality. It does not establish individual-edge necessity or distinguish maintenance from consequences of a stronger initial disruption. Reporting immediate attenuation does not control it. Also, cutting the whole exterior is a **benchmark, not a bound**: [the smoke](D:/Claude/random/wormWars/runs/memory-m4a-smoke/count_matched.txt:3) already has a random subset causing more tick-60 loss than the whole exterior.

4. **The smoke’s eligibility and class labels are invalid.** [The reader](D:/Claude/random/wormWars/scripts/memory_m4/m4a_read.py:78) imports census eligibility and classes by index despite these being different genomes. Recompute eligibility from the smoke’s own intact start; omit class interpretations without classification. The all-genome diagnostics still motivate changing direction.

5. **Part 2 cannot stay unchanged in implementation.** [Its aggregation](D:/Claude/random/wormWars/scripts/memory_m4/m4a_read.py:145) checks only whether a genome matched *any* order, then counts all three control outcomes—including unmatched orders that performed no cut. Save validity per order, exclude unmatched orders, and normalize contributions per genome with explicit denominators. Add a failing test for partial matching. Root-start assays remain appropriate, but their matched-control comparison remains conditional on coverage.
