**Revise.** Two things that can make R6 or the classes come out wrong, plus two smaller ones.

1. **`returns` needs an integer lag in substeps, but periods are not integers.** The planted three-cycle's period is about 2.4 ticks, 153.6 substeps at 64. With lag 154, consecutive returns drift 0.4 substeps apart, about 0.8% of range for a pure sine and more for a sharper waveform, against a 1% tolerance. Real cycles with τ near 0.5 drift more. So genuine cycles, the planted one included, can fail by construction. Compare at a fractional lag with interpolation, or widen the tolerance to max(1% of range, the largest one-substep change in the tail). The current test uses an exact period of 12; add one with 12.5 so it fails first.

2. **`classify` does not follow the plan's precedence.** In the both-located branch, an error bound above 10⁻⁹ returns "unresolved (ill-conditioned)" before the oscillation check (`classify.py:81`). A history whose tick-2000 Newton lands on an ill-conditioned root while the other is a confirmed oscillation gets "unresolved", not "persistent oscillation". Move the oscillation test ahead of every unresolved return, and add that case to `test_rules`.

3. **The recorded settling ranges are per genome, not per history.** `probe_states` uses `_Range`, which maxes over rows (`twin.py:35`); `per_row_range` is defined but unused. The candidate rule is per history. Compute it from the tail record.

4. **The continuation runs every located strain to the cap when any root is unstable or not near**, and the forecast overflows to inf/NaN there. Skip strains whose verdict is already fixed without it, and clip the forecast. The 64-substep rerun's length and compared stretch should also be stated (the period search needs at least four periods, 200 ticks at L = 50).
