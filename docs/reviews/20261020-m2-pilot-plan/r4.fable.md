**Revise**, but narrowly: three concrete fixes, none of which reopens the design.

1. **The 10⁻¹² floor sits below the root-accuracy gate (10⁻⁹).** For a fast root (tick radius 0.9) T is the minimum 200, the forecast at the last ticks is about d₀ · 0.9²⁰⁰ ≈ 7 × 10⁻¹⁴, and ten times that is under the floor, so the bound is 10⁻¹². The float64 continuation converges to the true root, which the gate lets sit up to 10⁻⁹ from the located one. So the common, fast case fails "reached" whenever e exceeds 10⁻¹². R4's 10⁻⁸ perturbation has the same gap: forecast at horizon 10⁻¹¹, bound 10⁻¹⁰, root error up to 10⁻⁹. Fix: floor = max(10⁻¹², 10 e) in both rules, or tighten the gate. The spike's 10⁻¹⁵ residuals mean it may rarely bite, but the rule contradicts its own gate.

2. **Code: the three-failure give-up is not sticky** (`scripts/memory_m2/equilibria.py:87-90`). `done` is rebuilt from `res < tol` every iteration and `stuck` marks only that iteration's failures, so a strain dropped at iteration i is re-admitted at i+2, solves and fails identically, and `failed_line_searches` and `iterations` climb past the plan's "three". The verdict is unaffected (deterministic), the counts and the early exit are. Keep a persistent `gave_up` mask; test it.

3. **Oscillation is confirmed vacuously when Newton locates no root from the orbit.** "No root located … is stable or marginal" passes on Newton failure from both starts. Name that "unresolved (no root located from the orbit)", and record the root's distance from the orbit's mean, since Newton from the mean can land on a root unrelated to the cycle. Also, "a limit cycle surrounds an unstable root" is planar; in N dimensions a stable cycle can coexist with a stable root, so the unresolved verdict there is conservative. Say so.
