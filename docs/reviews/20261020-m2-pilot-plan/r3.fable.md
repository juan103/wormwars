**Revise.** The "reached" rule, as written, fails by construction for most stable roots. The rest is implementable, and the module's conventions check out against `Brain.step`.

1. **"Reached" compares a 100-tick window against the forecast at the window's end.** The window-max over ticks T−99…T is about d₀ ρ^(T−99), while the bound is 10 d₀ ρ^T. With T = 200 and ρ^T = 10⁻³, ρ^(−99) ≈ 30, so the window-max is 3× the bound and a perfectly normal, converging root fails. Working it through: every root whose tick radius lies in roughly [0.90, 0.977] and whose forecast sits above the 10⁻¹² floor fails. A lone neuron at τ = 20 has ρ ≈ 0.951, so this band is the common case. Fix: bound each tick against its own forecast, or against d₀ ρ^(T−100). R4 uses the final distance and does not have this problem.

2. **ρ^T ignores non-normal transient growth, which is what M1 found in N2.** ‖P^(32t)‖ can exceed ρ^(32t) by the eigenbasis's condition number for a long while; the growers group is selected for exactly that, so its roots would fall to "located, not reached" for the forecast's sake. Cheap fix: forecast with the actual propagator norm, ‖P^(32t)‖₂ d₀, by repeated squaring (300×300, about 20 products). That also fixes finding 1.

3. **A confirmed oscillation with a P-stable Newton root is a contradiction the plan does not name.** The 90% persistence rule tolerates decay up to about 5 × 10⁻⁶ per tick, inside P's marginal band only by coincidence (3.2 × 10⁻⁵). State that a candidate whose located root is P-stable is unresolved, not oscillating, and record the pairing.

4. **Code, `equilibria.py`:** the backtracking accepts the step after 30 halvings even when the residual did not fall (the `lam > 1e-9` guard clears `worse`), so a failed line search is counted as progress; record it. `torch.linalg.solve` on an exactly singular A raises and aborts the whole batch; catch it or fall back to `lstsq`. The P = I + D Aᵀ, the continuous diag(1/τ) Aᵀ and the autograd column layout are correct; h = 1/k matches `BrainConfig.dt`.
