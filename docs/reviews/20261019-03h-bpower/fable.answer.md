**3. **4. Why B has no power is structural, not statistical.** The hypothesis predicts N2 at most 0.76 null-SDs above the null mean (0.0077 / 0.0102); the rank test requires about 1.7 to 2.1 SDs. Replacing the rank test with "N2's U minus the ensemble mean > 0 by its interval" would reach perhaps 0.4 to 0.5 power at u = 0, but it ignores graph heterogeneity, which the rank test exists to respect: about a fifth of null graphs would pass it.
