**redesign B**

1. **The rank calculation is correct; the claimed certainty is not.** [`b_power.py`](/D:/Claude/random/wormWars/experiments/03h-history-use/development-records/b_power.py:37) uses the right sign and cutoff: at most two nulls at or above N2. Its inputs match `pilot.json`, and the quoted rates match `b-power.json`. These are **rank-component scenario probabilities**, not power for B’s complete conjunctive claim. Under the model, complete-B power can only be lower. I inspected these outputs without rerunning them.

2. **Three graphs cannot establish the tail that determines this test.** The Gaussian assumption extrapolates an approximately 96th-percentile threshold from three observations. Normality of a within-graph average does not imply normality across graphs. Skewness, mixtures and heterogeneous variances could materially change the result. The chi-square SD bounds are algebraically correct **under iid Gaussian sampling**, but are not robust uncertainty bounds for power; the model also fixes the poorly estimated mean. Thus 0.47% at \(u=0\) is a concerning scenario, not an established forecast. Even its lower-SD scenario gives 22%.

3. **The noise conversion is unjustified.** [`b_readings()`](/D:/Claude/random/wormWars/wormwars/e03h/pilot.py:60) estimates the shared-world SE of the *three-graph mean*. Multiplying it by √3 recovers a common per-graph SE only under equal variances and zero covariance—neither established here—and still does not establish N2’s SE. For rankings, the relevant uncertainty includes
   \[
   \operatorname{Var}(\hat U_{N2}-\hat U_g)
   =V_{N2}+V_g-2\operatorname{Cov}(\hat U_{N2},\hat U_g).
   \]
   Common additive world noise cancels. The script’s statement that ignoring correlation “favours B” is not generally justified. Saved per-world arrays could support a better covariance analysis without GPU work, although N2’s covariance remains unmeasured.

4. **Selection uncertainty and “harm” need different wording.** Each condition selects its own decile. Consequently, U mixes treatment response with differences in selected membership; the reported overlaps are 23/26, 25/26 and 25/26. High reliability of fitness levels does not establish reliability of this small difference. The null SD already mixes topology, genome-draw, selection and test uncertainty; N2 receives only an assumed test-noise term. That is defensible only as a scenario conditional on a specified N2 draw and selection outcome, not unconditional prospective power. Also, \(U>0\) means relief underperforms **sham**, not necessarily level. Calling it “relief harms N2,” or treating \(u=0\) as a biological upper bound, overstates what this contrast means.

5. **I recommend the smallest statistical redesign: test mean advantage shrinkage directly.** Define
   \[
   \Delta=U_{N2}-\overline U_{\mathrm{SH\text{-}mirror\text{-}ASK}}.
   \]
   Use shared-world resampling and ensemble-graph resampling, conditioning explicitly on the genome draws and frozen selections, as the existing [interval framework specifies](/D:/Claude/random/wormWars/experiments/03h-history-use/DESIGN.md:257). Retain the initial-advantage and positive-relief gates; retain the rank as secondary. This could materially improve sensitivity because it tests N2 against the **ensemble mean**, rather than requiring an exceptional rank. It changes the inferential claim and must not be presented as evidence that N2 is exceptional among graph draws. A dated, owner-approved amendment before fresh formal data can legitimately make this change after the pilot. **Another GPU pilot is not automatically necessary for this statistical redesign**, though its uncertainty calculation needs review; the present files cannot establish its power.

6. **If exceptional rank is essential, there is no demonstrated analysis-only rescue.** Increasing test worlds cannot remove genuine graph or selection heterogeneity, and a common-decile statistic changes the target without demonstrated benefit. Then a new manipulation pilot is appropriate; amplitude 0.5 at the existing ASK site is the smallest intervention change, but doubling amplitude need not improve signal relative to heterogeneity. It requires a new amendment/binding: the existing [0.5 escalation rule](/D:/Claude/random/wormWars/experiments/03h-history-use/PILOT.md:190) was **not triggered**. I would not stop on the claim that random brains cannot respond: the selected-decile shift was detected, the all-genome average addresses another target, and the turn diagnostic measures only one tick.

7. **If option 1 is chosen, disclose weakly established power explicitly.** State that B had no registered power gate; the Gaussian scenarios suggest poor rank sensitivity for \(u\le0\); their tail, covariance and selection assumptions are unresolved; N2 was absent from the pilot; and full-B power was not calculated. Report Δ, component advantages, intervals and rank regardless of significance. “Not established” must not become “no memory-related head start.” The run could still yield useful estimates, but the pilot does not justify calling it a well-powered confirmatory test.
