# Review: the M4d plan, the transplant screen (round 1, design only)

Read-only, in the WormWars repository. After M4c (D362), you both recommended M4's exploratory transplant screen
(`docs/reviews/20261024-m4-next/r1.fable.md`, `r1.astra.md`). This is the plan written from your points:
`docs/memory/M4D-PLAN.md` (draft 1). No code yet; it is reviewed before coding, so design changes are cheap now.

How draft 1 answers your points:
- density: arms D (N2's 260 edges at random positions, weights shuffled), R (rewired within the block at fixed
  in/out degrees, weights with their edges) and P (N2's positions, weights shuffled), all with T's count, weight
  multiset and autapses; T against R is the primary contrast, T against I required with it;
- fresh hosts from 03's samplers (two per ensemble, ten in all), checked against earlier names and content; hosts are
  the bootstrap unit;
- untouched parameters: one genome panel per host drawn on the intact host, every arm's genome built from it (outside
  edges, tau, bias and g kept; block magnitudes on the host's scale, which equals N2's because every sampler permutes
  N2's weights; one shared 30×30 sign matrix per genome); a sham must reproduce `Genome.random` exactly;
- the read-out trap: every drawn genome classified, no eligibility floor; the primary measure is the share of all 1024
  draws classified separate states (both subclasses);
- write, hold and read reported per arm; T+G (N2's gap junctions in the block) secondary; Fable's circuitry companion
  secondary and descriptive;
- staged and bounded: full C3 only; smaller blocks, placement away from the read-out, matched non-C3 blocks and the
  reverse are deferred to a second stage planned after this reading.

to 6 autapses and 6 to 15 gap pairs there, with exactly N2's chemical weight multiset. Under the N2 configuration,
Dale's law is off and magnitudes are anatomical. M2's census found 937 to 1010 eligible genomes of 1024 per panel.
