**Recommendation.** Do not run E4's main formal run. Publish the pilot, the diagnostics and stage A as E4's exploratory result, and make E4-D the confirmatory experiment: first its CPU feasibility check as designed, then an evolutionary E4-D with its own pre-registration. **1. Reading 1 is right in its conclusion, wrong in one part of its mechanism.** "There is little information in A that B can use" over-reads stage A. What the files show is narrower and stronger:

- The cost is a sensitivity, not an information effect. Cross-link-only children at 0.25 lose 3.39 visits. The constant part alone (mean-substituted) loses 2.64 and the fluctuating part alone (centred) loses 2.99. Those do not add; at factor 1.0 the mean-substituted twin (1.93) is worse than intact (2.07). Both halves are past a breaking threshold, so reading 2's test cannot separate "wrong gradient" from "any input of this size".
- Why A's noses cost most has a mechanical reason you can check in the module and the world: scent is truncated at 9 grid cells, so during most of a B-leg B's own noses read zero, while A's noses are strong because the worm has just left A. Into a comparator with gain 3 and zero own drive, a link of σ 0.02 (0.08 × 0.25) on tanh activities near 0.7 moves the turn command by a sizeable fraction of the carrier's 0.2. That is why the within-module twin (weights only, no cost in the diagnostics) is harmless: B's own nose edges carry nothing when B's scent is out of range.
- The consequence that matters: the weight at which a cross-link is functional and the weight at which a random-signed one breaks B are the same, about 0.01 to 0.02. Extrapolating the ladder (about 1.2 visits per doubling at the low end), the registered load bound of 0.25 visits is met near factor 0.02 to 0.03, σ 0.002, where a lineage's 22 to 57 mutations drift about 0.01 to 0.015. So any factor that passes the bound cannot reach a functional weight by drift, and selection on 8 noisy mazes (per-maze SD 3.7) is too weak to accumulate signed steps. A null P1 and P2 follow by construction. Agreed on "poor value".
- One caveat on "nothing to transfer": an anti-gradient to A (leave the previous goal while B's scent is out of range) is real information A holds and B lacks. Whether it could help depends on headroom between E-G and the oracle on E4's mazes, which is not measured anywhere I can find (`docs/E4/eg-check.json` has the seed, E-G and the walk only). Say "no headroom is expected" only after the oracle is played on one block, a few CPU minutes.

**2. Reading 2 is half right.** The rule was not ill-posed; its premise was. Astra wrote "evaluate the combined mutation mask" and "95% of unmutated E-G", so the combined load was meant to be regulated. What nobody expected was that weights and τ at 0.25 cost 7% on block A and 6% on block B when the diagnostics' 8-maze block showed them costing nothing (4.14 and 4.16 against 4.03). Absolute losses scale with E-G's level on a block (4.03 there, 7.97 here), so fixed-visit and percent tolerances are block-dependent. "Reconsider explicitly" is the right outcome. If any further training ever uses this mask, Fable's half of the rule picks 0.02 and the 93% is declared as the operating point. It is moot under (ii).

**3. Artefacts in stage A.** None that change the conclusion. To report:

- The means hide bimodality. Share of children at or above 5 visits: 42% at cross 0.25, 28% for nose biases, 100% for bias factors 0.02 and below. The "load" is mostly the fraction of children that break. Report that share beside every mean.
- The cross-link ladder with frozen biases is not an artefact: the cross-link-only condition, everything inherited frozen, reproduces the cost (3.39 against the ladder's 2.87 increment).
- Pairing and seeds are correct in `scripts/e4_calibrate.py` (same replicate seed, `mutate` draws identical noise with or without scales). Compositions differ between E-G (1 strain) and children (116 strains), which matters at float precision, not at visits.
- The ladder never measured below 0.25, so "every factor fails" is true of the registered candidates and the ladder, not of 0.03. Do not fill that gap; point 1 says it would pass only where it is useless.

1. **(ii) E4-D as the confirmatory question.** Can conclude, with an exact structural chance level of 0.5, whether selection discovers an A to B link when it is necessary and strongly selected. Cannot say anything about transfer in a natural shuttle, since the cue is engineered, nor about consolidation.
