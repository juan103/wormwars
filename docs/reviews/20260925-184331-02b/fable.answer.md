## MAJOR

**1. The R1 control cannot separate "N2 structure" from "the amphid food route", and the 0.84 is partly read-out-side.** RESULTS.md:11-18, 52-71, D048. R1 enters through ASJ, ASI and ASG, amphid chemosensory neurons whose first-order targets are AIA, AIB and AIY, the same layer AWC and ASE feed. The motor read-out is identical under both mappings. So AIY, AIZ and RIA staying critical is exactly what the food-route reading predicts. Note also where the largest single drops are: IL1VR, URYVL, RIML (criticality.json:55-68, 133), IL1VL 2.02 (line 569), URXL, IL2L, CEPVR (lines 1021-1056), RIH 2.11, CEPDR, URYDR, OLLR, URBR (lines 3564-3589). Those are head neurons that synapse or gap-couple onto RMD and SMD, the turn read-out. Criticality is measuring adjacency to the shared read-out plus the shared amphid pathway, not hubness. Cheapest discriminating check: the T1-R2 N2 champions already exist (PLN, IL2D, IL2V, non-amphid). Run `stage_criticality(mapping="R2", families=("N2",))`. Cost is 8 runs at about 51 s each (criticality.json "seconds"), so about 7 GPU-minutes. Zero-GPU companion: correlate mean drop with each neuron's summed anatomical weight onto the 18 read-out neurons.

**2. Generation 0 is the wrong control for "evolution builds history sensitivity".** RESULTS.md:21-24, 91, analyse.py:225-227, D048:1100. Mutation alone inflates the measure. With `w_sigma` 0.08, `p_mutate` 1.0 and 39 generations (config.py:185-189), the champion lineage accumulates about 0.5 of added weight SD on an initial scale of 0.2, and tau random-walks in log space by a factor of about 2.5. Larger recurrent gain and slower neurons both raise an absolute output difference without any selection. The measure also conflates gain with memory: the g39 turn read-out spans a far wider range than g00 (history.json:93-171 vs 6-84). Two cheap fixes. First, a mutation-only drift control: apply `Genome.mutate` 39 times to each g00 champion with the run's MutationConfig, then run `history_one`. No rollouts, seconds per champion. Second, a gain-free statistic: normalise by the equilibrated output contrast between constant 0.25f and constant 1.75f, or use the already-saved 5-tick post-traces to report the fraction of the difference that survives. For run00 g39 it is 0.729 → 0.714 after 5 ticks, which reads as distinct attractors or very slow modes, not a decaying trace. Related: 30 warm ticks from a zero state (analyse.py:186, 208) is under two time constants at `tau_max` 20, so the "constant" condition is itself not at steady state.

**3. The forward row of the history table is censored by the motor clamp.** analyse.py:183, RESULTS.md:84-89. `forward_rising_minus_falling` is exactly 0.0 whenever forward sits at 1.0 in all three histories: T0 N2 g39 runs 01, 02, 04 (history.json:514, 858, 1546), 7 of 16 T0 SH g39, 6 of 16 T0 SH g00, and 3 of 8 T1 N2 g39. The "runs above 0" counts are therefore "uncensored vs censored", and the direction-split reading rests on five values. Report the raw read-out before gain and clip, as `input_response` already does with its "raw" keys.

**4. "Evolved N2 champions steer toward food; shuffles do not" is two champions, and the world data disagree.** RESULTS.md:24-28, 123-129, D048:1102. The g39 N2 mean +0.0093 is run00 (+0.0375) and run02 (+0.0440) (response.json:27, 147). The other six are within ±0.008, three negative. Five of eight positive at g39 versus three of eight at g00, n = 8, no interval. The absolute directional response did not grow (0.0120 → 0.0128). Meanwhile three SH champions lose 0.27 to 0.38 to the swap in the world (response.json:179, 419, 509) with probe responses of 0.011, 0.0035 and 0.0019, and the in-world regression gives the same L−R turn coefficient for SH (0.22) as N2 (0.23). The probe misses in-world side dependence, and the summary picks the probe. Say "two of eight N2 champions show a directional probe response; side dependence in the world is present in both groups". Cheap improvement: run the probe at each champion's operating point (typical food 0.3 to 0.5 in history.json, not `base` 0.1).

**5. Turn-sign persistence is computed across weys, not across ticks.** analyse.py:160. `d` is a concatenation of per-tick blocks of all alive weys (lines 133-137), so `d[1:]` against `d[:-1]` compares adjacent weys at the same tick for 159 of every 160 rows (8 worlds × 20 weys). The 0.91, 0.98 and 0.998 at RESULTS.md:117 measure "fraction of neighbouring weys sharing a turn sign". The "N2 a little less" reading is an artefact, though an interesting one: the two low values, run00 0.67 and run02 0.65 (behaviour.json:24, 120), are exactly the two probe steerers, whose weys split in turn direction. Log a wey id and compute it per wey, as `probes.behaviour` does (probes.py:166).

**6. The section-5 Spearman mishandles ties, so 0.35 is a lower bound.** analyse.py:73-75. Argsort-of-argsort assigns arbitrary ranks within ties. Anatomical weights are EM section counts (integers, heavily tied). At g00 the tie structure of |w| is identical so 1.0 is exact; at g39 the within-tie ranks of anat are arbitrary while |w| is not, which deflates the correlation. Recompute with average ranks. Drop "two thirds of the rank information is gone" (RESULTS.md:141) either way. This erosion is also what mutation alone predicts, which RESULTS rightly does not contradict.

**7. The 03a implication is the kept-connection rule, not just SC3's mirror baseline.** RESULTS.md:68-71, 148. 03a keeps each target's true connections to interface neurons fixed in every arm (03a DRAFT.md:235-239). If the high-criticality panel is dominated by read-out-adjacent neurons (point 1), their criticality is carried by exactly the edges that are kept, so NIP reinsertion recovers the score regardless of hidden partners and the headroom criterion collapses. Cheapest check: a `delete_neurons` variant that zeros only edges between X and mapped neurons, compared with full deletion for the top 12 N2 targets. One rollout per target, under a minute.
