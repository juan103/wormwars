**Verdict: revise (minor).** The design resolves the coverage failure and keeps D1's measure; two small changes before it runs.

1. **Balance: "heavy" is weighted by the wrong thing.** The standardised share weights used strata by π, which in `summary.json` is uniform (0.105 each, 0.053 for stratum 9). A used stratum holding 2% of N2's numerator still carries a tenth of the standardised share. Gating only strata with ≥5% of N2's used numerator repeats M1's mismatch in a milder form. Since every used stratum already has ≥10 N2 genomes by construction, apply the 25% bound to **every used stratum** (or gate on a π-weighted mean). With stratum widths of about 1.5× in a, 25% is not vacuous but is only violated by a pile-up at a stratum edge, so this is cheap and honest. Keep 25%.

2. **Coverage now passes almost by construction**, so say so. With k = 1/64 and near-linear a(k), any genome with a(1) ≤ 5.5 lands in a used stratum; the plan's numbers (2.57, 1.96) sit well inside. The reading then rests entirely on the caveat "matched a, mismatched k". The caveats are adequate, but the r(1/64)/r(1/32) and r(1/8)/r(1/4) ratios should carry a **pre-stated annotation band** (say outside [0.8, 1.25] annotates the reading as "beyond linearisation"), not a gate. Float32 rounding at 1/64 is not a concern at these magnitudes: a genome at the floor still has per-neuron differences thousands of ulps above float32 resolution at |v| ~ 1; the ratio will show it if I am wrong.
