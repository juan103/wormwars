**1. Right fix, with two additions (MAJOR).** The odour is the correct conceptual change: today the signal and the reward are the same field, so the reward is wherever the signal is and no gradient is needed. The bump profile inside a patch is a gradient already, and it is useless because eating works at the edge. An instantaneous mass-preserving blur of food plus pellet, recomputed each tick, is the cleanest version. It tracks depletion with no lag and no warm-up, unlike a diffuse-and-decay channel, and it is cheaper than anything analytic that would have to weight patch centres by remaining food. Two things must go with it. First, the economy change above. Second, the controller families in the gate must nest (see 3). The current OneStepMemory has constant speed and cannot express kinesis, so it lost on a widened grid partly by construction, and StereoProportional cannot slow down on food for the same reason.

**2. New failure modes.**
- Kinesis on a smooth field (MAJOR). Orthokinesis with speed decreasing in concentration aggregates at high concentration on its own, with steady-state density proportional to 1 over speed. Blur helps kinesis as well as taxis. Thresholded stopping now stops at an odour contour that may be off the edible patch, which hurts kinesis, but the tuned version will just raise its threshold. The margin is quantitative and only the gate can settle it.
- All patches merge into one bump (MAJOR at today's map). With patch centres inside a central box of about 8 cells and S above 2, the odour is a single blob centred on the arena. T1 becomes "find the middle". Spread the patches and enlarge the arena before choosing S.
- Signal scale (MAJOR). Blur preserves mass, so peak sensed value drops by roughly patch area over (patch area plus 2 pi S squared), about 3 to 4 times at S = 3. Food is already the weakest channel against collision at scale 2.0 (design audit: 0.14 to 0.39 versus about 1.1). Re-tune sense_scale_food so the odour peak sits near today's food peak, and do that before calibration.
- Walls (MINOR). The blur zero-pads, so odour falls toward walls. That adds no spurious maxima, only a mild push away from walls that every controller shares. Fine.
- Diffusion speed (MINOR, avoided). With an instantaneous blur there is no dynamics and no stale odour. Do not use diffuse_decay for this.
- Probe code (MAJOR, small fix). `_probe_food` at `wormwars/world.py:451` samples the raw food and pellet channels for the mirrored probe, and `_food_constant` at line 300 uses the raw food mean. Both must use the odour, or the mirrored signal will have a sharper, sparser distribution than the real one and the "same distribution" claim fails.
- S versus body speed (MINOR). Per-tick change at full speed is about 0.35 / S of the local level, so 12 percent at S = 3. A one-tick comparator sees that easily and tau_min of 0.5 ticks lets the evolved brains differentiate over one to three ticks. S in 2 to 4 is the sensible range; larger flattens the field.
- Cost (MINOR). A 19 by 19 kernel on a 24 by 24 grid per world per tick is far below the brain's 302-neuron, 32-substep update. A larger arena raises field cost a few times; re-run the timed pilot.

**3. The gate, exactly.**
- Families, all reading the same scaled current the brains get, with one threshold on level c and piecewise-constant outputs:
  K (memoryless mono): v(c) in {v_lo, v_hi}, omega(c) in {w_lo, w_hi}, threshold theta.
  M (memory mono): K plus a turn term on the one-tick or short-EMA change, omega += k_d times clip(minus delta c / theta_d, 0, 1), optionally v also gated on the sign of the change. With k_d = 0 it is K, so M never loses to K on tuning worlds and the held-out gap is the value of memory.
  S (stereo memoryless): K plus k_s (c_L minus c_R). Also nests K.
  S+M: for interpretation only, to see whether T0 also rewards memory.
- Grids: v in {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1}, omega in {0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.4, 0.7, 1}, thresholds at quantiles of the sensed odour distribution measured on tuning worlds, k_d and k_s over four values. Rule, written down: any parameter at a grid edge that is not a physical bound (v = 1, omega = 0) forces a widened rerun. That is the lesson of this round. Use 64 or more tuning worlds, since a grid of thousands of points on 32 worlds has a winner's curse of about 0.07 in score.
- Worlds: tune on TUNING_IDS, evaluate on the same 1408 held-out worlds the analysis normalises against, paired per world, bootstrap over worlds as now in memory_vs_memoryless.
- Margin: state it as memory's share of the best controller's advantage over the straight runner, (M minus K) / (M minus straight), at least one third with a bootstrap lower bound above one fifth. A share this size is what probe 2 will have to detect on evolved champions. Add a headroom check: the best controller's eaten fraction on the median world below 0.9, or the ceiling is binding and the margin is capped.
- T0: (S minus K) / (S minus straight) by the same rule, and S at least equal to M within its interval, so T0 is solvable without memory while T1 is not. If S+M beats S by the same share, say so in the pre-registration, because then the task contrast is partly memory versus memory.
- Validate the champion probe on the scripted controllers (MAJOR). The current temporal check at `wormwars/exp02/analysis.py:161` fires on constant and mirrored food, which test "uses food at all", not "uses history". Kinesis fails both too. Add a history-only ablation, for example sample-and-hold of the mono signal for 8 to 16 ticks, or an EMA with a 30-tick constant. Under it, scripted K must be unchanged within its interval and scripted M must fall toward K. Only a probe that passes this check can back the tripwire "T1 champions do not depend on food history".
