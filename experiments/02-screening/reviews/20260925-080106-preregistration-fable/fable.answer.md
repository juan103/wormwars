I read the pre-registration, D034-D040, the exploration README, Astra's three reviews, the report/analysis/probe code, the world's sensing path, the CLI, the validation JSON, and the running bundle. Findings, most severe first.

**MAJOR**

1. **Q4, unnamed failure mode: the primary contrast is a test of mirror symmetry, not of the C. elegans wiring.** A degree-preserving shuffle destroys L/R pairing: AWCL and AWCR keep their out-degrees but their targets are unrelated (`wormwars/connectome/graphs.py:93-97`), while the turn read-out is itself bilateral. Any bilaterally symmetric graph computes L minus R almost for free; any asymmetric one has to evolve it. That predicts N2 > SH on the bilateral-mean probe regardless of anything specific to the connectome. Worse, the intended control does not touch it: R1 and R2 are also symmetric pairs inside N2 (`remaps.json`), so under remaps N2 still has its symmetry. The pre-registration's reading "similar advantage under remaps weakens the M0-specific reading" (§4, line 106) is exactly the symmetry signature, but it is never named as such. Cheap fix before probes, no evolution: compute per graph (N2, SH1-8) a mirror-symmetry index, e.g. the share of chemical edges preserved under the L↔R relabelling, and the Jaccard overlap of the AWCL and AWCR out-neighbourhoods; register it as a reported covariate and add one sentence to §4: "a supported primary with a comparable advantage under R1/R2 is read as a symmetry effect, and the full design needs a mirror-symmetric shuffle control".

2. **Q2, jitter on T0 is a stereo ablation, not a history one.** Jitter displaces every sample point independently (`world.py:483-488`), including P_FRONT_L and P_FRONT_R, which sit 1 cell apart (`configs/interface.yaml:17`, lateral offset 0.5). Jitter of radius 1 therefore injects L−R noise of the same order as the signal after a sigma-1 blur. The validation ran on T1 only (`scripts/exp02.py:234`, `c1 = task_config("T1")`), where mono sensing makes this invisible. §5 lists jitter 1 and 3 on T0 as "history sensitivity". On T0 they measure stereo plus history sensitivity, and a champion that is a pure stereo comparator will look "history-sensitive". Fix: run `_history_probe_validation` on the T0 config with S and K (scripted, minutes) and record it in `probe_validation.json`; if K or S moves under jitter 1 on T0, relabel the T0 jitters in §5 as "spatial-noise sensitivity".

3. **Q1, "challenged" merges two opposite findings.** `prediction_verdict` (`analysis.py:252-256`) returns the same label when N2's use interval is below 0.10 and when Delta is reversed. The first says the 40-generation search found stereo for nobody (the pilot shuffles' stereo use was 0.02-0.07, so this is the single most likely outcome); the second says N2's wiring is worse than a shuffle's. §10 treats them identically, but the label will be read as evidence against N2. Register two sub-labels with no change to the rule: "challenged, no meaningful N2 use" and "challenged, contrast reversed". Otherwise the verdict rule is sound and consistent with the code; the conjunction needs no multiplicity correction.

**MINOR**

4. **Q2, the 95% intervals are nominal.** Percentile bootstrap of a mean from 8 units (`paired_bootstrap_many`, `analysis.py:101-117`) undercovers, typically 88-92% for near-normal data and worse with a skewed run or one strong graph. Not fixable without units; say in §4 that coverage is nominal, and report a t-interval next to it.

5. **Q2, the "64 probe worlds" are 64 different worlds per unit.** Maps depend on (run_seed, id) (`bundle.json` seed_scheme), and probes use the run's seed (`scripts/exp02.py:510`). So N2 and SH are scored on disjoint world sets, and `sh_graphs_with_use` averages world i of run 0 with world i of run 1, which are different worlds; its docstring "(the same probe worlds)" (`report.py:140-142`) is false. The bootstrap remains valid because each index is an independent draw, so this is wording, not a defect. A common probe seed would pair the groups on worlds, but the gain is small since U_p is already within-champion paired, and it would touch the registered completeness rule. Fix the wording in §4/§7 and the docstring.

6. **Q2, mean-probe estimand needs the swap to be read.** A one-sided reader (uses L only) is changed by the mean probe by (R−L)/2 without comparing anything. Only the swap separates a comparator (reversed, worse than K, as `S_change` shows) from a one-sided reader (unchanged in expectation). Add one sentence to §4: meaningful use under the mean with no loss under the swap is sample-point sensitivity, not a comparison.

7. **Q4, SH graphs carry shortcuts N2 is denied.** With food-neuron out-degrees of 10-17 and 18 read-out neurons among 302, each shuffled food neuron has roughly 0.5-1 expected direct edges onto the read-out, and the shuffle also permutes magnitudes (`graphs.py:97`), so some are heavy. D036 enforces ≤2 direct weight for N2's remaps only. Report, per SH graph, the same six routing features D036 computes for the food pairs. It costs seconds and is the obvious covariate for the per-graph detection count.

8. **Q4, probe the g79 champions.** Continuation runs save g79 genomes (`scripts/exp02.py:330-338`) but `cmd_probes` loads only g00 and g39 (`line 496`). Four T0-M0 units at ~1.4 min each would directly inform §10's "needs longer evolution" branch. Add as exploratory.

9. **Q3, the anchor is 01b's world, not 01b's procedure.** 01b ran 8 substeps, 25 generations, 32 held-out worlds and 24-genome calibration (`runs/exp01b-direction-corrected/bundle.json`); the anchor runs 32 substeps, 40 generations, 64 worlds and the D035 calibration. The tripwire's stated consequence should be "01b's sign did not survive the procedure change", not a bug flag. Nothing in the evolution warrants stopping: the bundle is clean at 225e8f8, seeds are crossed, the g39 snapshot is chosen by the same rule for 40- and 80-generation runs (`evolve.py:130-131`), and probe ids are disjoint from training and held-out ranges.

10. **Q2, normalisation is a mean of per-world ratios** (`analysis.py:53`), so food-poor worlds where the best script scores near zero dominate the fitness interactions. Secondary only; report raw alongside or use a ratio of means.

**Fine:** the estimand definitions, equal graph weighting, the nested resampling, seed 0 and 20 000 resamples match §4; `paired_diff` gates every consumer; completeness checks registered runs, seeds, world ids and finiteness; the mean/swap act before scaling and clamp; `food_constant` on T0 correctly removes stereo too, and is labelled "uses food at all"; the budget stages and "not assessed" path read correctly; the anchor's empty-cell resampling is handled.

**Recap.** Nothing requires stopping the evolution. Before any probe runs I would do three cheap things: add the symmetry index and its registered reading (point 1), validate jitter 1 on T0 with the scripted S and K (point 2), and split the "challenged" label (point 3). Points 5, 6, 7 and 8 are wording and free covariates; do them in the same commit.
