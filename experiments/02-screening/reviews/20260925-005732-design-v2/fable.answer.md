## 1. Failure modes not yet on the radar, ranked by expected damage

**1. MAJOR. The swarm's own pheromone is a stereo food beacon, so T1 does not remove spatial information and the remaps do not remove the amphid route.** In single-swarm foraging, `ally_pheromone` is sampled left and right at the front points (`wormwars/world.py:463`), from a channel every living wey tops up by 1.0 per tick at its own position (`world.py:751`). With decay 0.93 the cell under a wey parked on food climbs toward roughly 14, times sense scale 0.35 hits the input clamp of 5. Food itself is 70 to 160 units spread over 20 to 80 cells, so the food current is around 1. The pheromone left by teammates who found food is therefore a stronger, stereo, spatially resolved cue than the food signal. The body-density collision channel does the same at lower gain. Consequences: T1's mono restriction is on the food channel only, and the evolved controller can steer on pheromone stereo without any temporal computation, so "T1 demands memory" would pass on the scripted controllers and be false for the evolved networks. Worse for the main question: pheromone enters through ASK, an amphid neuron projecting into the same AIA/AIB/AIY hub the food neurons use, and that route is untouched by M1 to M3. The mapping contrast is diluted under every mapping in the same direction. Detection: the current tripwires cannot see it. Probes: evaluate every champion with each sensory channel silenced at test time (pheromone, body, hazard, collision), which is evaluation-only and cheap, and report the score drop per channel and per cell. Decide up front whether T1 sets `sense_scale_pheromone` to 0, and make the memoryless scripted controller use every stereo channel, not just food.

**2. MAJOR. Calibration on a sensor-silent probe measures a different operating point from the one evolution runs in, and the one-shot gain fit cannot hit an absolute target.** In-world, every wey injects a constant self-collision current: the body field splats head, mid and tail with blur 1 (`world.py:775`), and the front sample sits 0.6 cells ahead of the head, so `collision_front` is nonzero at every tick. Random populations also see food. A silent probe therefore yields lower raw drive, higher fitted gains (`calibration.py:136` is a single linear fit capped at 40), and more saturation of the clipped read-out (`world.py:499`) once sensory input arrives. The size of that shift depends on how strongly each graph routes ALM/AVM to the read-out, so it is graph-dependent. 01b already showed the linear fit lands at 84 to 97% of target; the design's 10% tripwire would have fired on 01b. Fixes: make `calibrate` iterate (two or three secant steps) so the achieved drive matches the target, calibrate in-world under M0 and freeze per graph (freezing is what gives mapping independence, silence is not needed), and put probe 4's in-world generation-0 drive per cell, not the probe drive, behind the tripwire. Also report the fitted gains and the fraction of ticks with saturated forward or turn output per champion. Because ticks, substeps, calibration and world count all change, T0 is not an anchor to 01b unless you add a tripwire: the T0-M0 N2 minus SH contrast on the speed measure should reproduce 01b's sign. If it flips, that is a finding about calibration, since 01b's edge was consistent with head start.

**3. MAJOR. The remap arithmetic does not close, and the matching misses convergence.** Three disjoint sets of three pairs need nine pairs; the candidate list has eight (ASI, ASJ, ASG, IL2D, IL2V, IL2, AWB, PLN). Either a tolerance is being relaxed or a set is short. Three of the eight are IL2 subclasses, heavily interwired, so if each set holds one IL2 pair the three remaps are correlated and the "remaps disagree in sign" tripwire loses meaning. Separately, AWA, AWC and ASE converge on the same interneurons, so M0 delivers three copies of the signal to one hub. Degree and hop distance do not match convergence, and a remap that spreads the signal over three unrelated hubs differs from M0 in input summation, not in specialisation. Probe: add a single-pair mapping (M0 with AWC only) as a convergence control, and report out-neighbourhood overlap within each triple. I could not recompute hop distances; before pre-registering, state whether "motor read-out" means the ten forward neurons, the eight turn neurons, or all eighteen, whether gap junctions were counted as bidirectional hops, and check specifically whether any IL2 or PLN member has a direct edge to RMDD/RMDV or SMD, which would make it a shortcut.

**4. MAJOR. Four SH graphs cannot estimate the graph-to-graph variance component, which is job 2.** The ratio of graph to run variance from 4 graphs and 2 runs each has about 3 and 4 degrees of freedom; the tripwire "graph variance dominates" will fire or not fire almost at random, and the hierarchical bootstrap in `stats.py:53` over 4 clusters has 35 distinct resamples, far too few. There is a free efficiency gain: SH preserves only degree, and the remaps are degree-matched, so SH under M0 and under Mk are near-exchangeable. The SH block of the interaction is a null correction, and its across-mapping spread is a free check of the matching. Swap: 8 SH graphs, 1 run each, in all 9 cells. Same 72 runs, graph variance from 7 degrees of freedom, run-to-run variance from N2's 6 runs per cell and from the SH across-mapping spread. Report the SH M0 versus Mk contrast explicitly as the matching check.

**5. MAJOR. The integrator tripwire will fire on chaos, not on resolution.** Fitness at 32 versus 128 substeps on the same worlds differs through sensitive dependence of trajectories on the brain state, even when the integrator is converged. "Any champion of 126" is a max statistic, so a 5% shift somewhere is near certain. Add a chaos floor: the same comparison at 128 versus 512, or 32 versus 32 with a 1e-6 perturbation of the initial state. Fire only if the 32 versus 128 shift exceeds the floor by a stated factor. Note that at 32 substeps the explicit chemical step gain in D032's formula is still about 12 at tau_min, so keep the semi-implicit option live.

**6. MAJOR. "Still improving between generations 30 and 40" is undefined on the training curve.** Training worlds are redrawn every generation (`rollout.py:127`), so best-of-generation fitness is non-monotone noise around a plateau, and about half of converged runs will look "improving" by chance. Use the held-out curve logged every 5 generations, test the paired gen-30 to gen-40 difference across runs per cell, and fit a saturating curve to report generations to 90% of asymptote, which is what sizes the full experiment. Also report the interaction at generation 0 and at generation 40 separately, since 01b's whole edge was head start.

**7. MINOR. The valence probe is correct in its logic and nearly powerless as designed.** Negating the food current is compensated by flipping the bias and all chemical in- and out-weights of the six food neurons, which is inside the initial and mutational distribution, so the only asymmetry is their gap junctions, as the design says. Four evolved runs per arm will never detect a gap-junction effect against run noise. A generation-0 probe over hundreds of strains, negated versus not, is exact, far more powerful and costs seconds. Separately, the argument disposes of "innate urge" but not of the reason a hazard task is interesting: the hazard channel enters through AFD, a different route through N2, which is exactly the "does it generalise across interfaces" question. Defer it explicitly rather than calling it uninformative.

**8. MINOR. The held-out score is bounded by world food, not by controller quality.** Total food is 210 to 960 against 280 of drain over 400 ticks and 560 of intake capacity, so in poor worlds a perfect forager scores below 0.7. This inflates world variance and makes "ceiling" from scripted controllers a poor reference; normalise per world by the best scripted controller's score on that world, and allow evolved scores above the scripted ceiling in the tripwire wording. The champion is picked on 8 training worlds (`evolve.py:165`), adding winner-selection noise to the held-out score; logging held-out for the top three elites and reporting their mean as a secondary measure is nearly free.

**9. MINOR, several.** D030 asks experiment 02 to decide the gap-junction truncation and the design does not. `compare` in `stats.py:94` resamples conditions independently, discarding the seed pairing the runner creates; the new bootstrap must keep run blocks across cells. Run cells interleaved and record per-run wall time, since 01b's cost figures tracked run order. The 30% margin is below the 2 to 5 times slowdowns 01b's own pre-registration recorded. "MS moves N2's advantage far more" and "remaps disagree in sign" need numeric thresholds relative to their intervals.

## 2. Is the fraction well chosen?

Mostly yes for jobs 1 and 3, no for job 2 as sized. Keep T0 and T1, keep M0 to M3, keep MS as the first cut. Make the SH swap in point 4. Add the channel-silencing evaluation and the 01b replication tripwire; both are evaluation-only and fit in the existing probe budget. Add the single-pair convergence control on T1 only if MS survives the cut.
