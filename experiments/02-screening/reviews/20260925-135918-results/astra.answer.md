1. **MAJOR — “Evolution erodes N2’s advantage” obscures what changed.** At [RESULTS.md:82](D:/Claude/random/wormWars/experiments/02-screening/RESULTS.md:82), comparing an interval excluding zero with one crossing zero does not test change. My **post-hoc** paired hierarchical bootstrap, 100,000 resamples, gives approximately −0.061 [−0.108, −0.015] for T0, but −0.039 [−0.096, +0.021] for T1. More revealing: N2’s own normalised M0-minus-remap contrast barely changes—T0 **0.02583 → 0.02562**, T1 **0.02842 → 0.02835**. The relative interaction shrinks because SH’s initial M0 deficit disappears. Report relative catch-up; do not imply selection removed N2’s routing mechanism. Also, these generation-0 fitness observations are **best-of-32 champions**, not average random brains (`wormwars/evo/evolve.py:130`).

Motor-response ratios are different again. “Only under M0 … outside the shuffle range” at line 112 is literally false: R2’s absolute response is **0.005005**, above SH’s maximum **0.004805**; MS falls below its SH range. Finally, this is a fixed-input, outside-world probe of random brains, not evolved champions (`wormwars/exp02/probes.py:81`). It cannot establish that evolution discarded this response.

5. **MAJOR — “Nobody” and “stereo steering is real but worth little” need narrower scope.** [RESULTS.md:70](D:/Claude/random/wormWars/experiments/02-screening/RESULTS.md:70) itself identifies T0-R1-SH1-run00 with bilateral-mean loss **0.427 [0.340, 0.519]** and swap loss **0.955 [0.830, 1.084]**. Swap sensitivity supports directional dependence, but does not uniquely identify a bilateral comparator rather than one-sided sampling.

6. **MAJOR — Food dependence supports an intervention effect, not stronger biological food processing.** The numbers at [RESULTS.md:116](D:/Claude/random/wormWars/experiments/02-screening/RESULTS.md:116) match, but “N2 reads food more strongly” overinterprets them. On T0-M0 probe worlds, real scores are **2.835 versus 2.742**; constant-food scores are **1.377 versus 1.742**. Much of the larger N2 ablation loss therefore reflects worse performance under the replacement. The constant is each world’s initial spatial mean, which changes the input distribution (`wormwars/exp02/probes.py:49`). Also disclose that the generation-0 T0 food-dependence interaction crosses zero: **+0.370 [−0.030, +0.758]**. Likewise, MS’s negative result establishes a mapping effect, not that direct edges themselves caused the cost; MS changes neuron identity and other routing properties together.

8. **MAJOR — The full-design precision claim understates uncertainty.** [RESULTS.md:187](D:/Claude/random/wormWars/experiments/02-screening/RESULTS.md:187)’s “within ±0.04” applies only to the point estimates. Do not use that boundary estimate to justify replacing graph replication with repeated runs.

10. **MAJOR — The full design should investigate the successful strategies before prescribing task redesign.** The omitted behaviour data show turn-sign persistence around **0.996–0.997** for T0-M0, and removing collision input costs approximately **0.404 N2 / 0.516 SH**, much more than removing the food difference. These are descriptive clues worth following, not proof of a mechanism. Test richer memoryless baselines with the same inputs, matched-current/different-history replay, and controlled steering responses of actual champions. Establish capability acquisition with the proposed search before expanding replication. Preserve symmetry and match routing in suitable controls rather than treating either structural covariate as causally resolved here.
